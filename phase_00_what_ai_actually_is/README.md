# Phase 0: What AI Actually Is

Welcome to **Phase 0** of the AI Complete Mastery Curriculum! 

Before we dive into heavy linear algebra, multidimensional calculus, probability density functions, and thousands of lines of neural network code, we must construct an unbreakable conceptual foundation. 

Most people use the word "AI" every day without actually knowing what it means. Is a calculator AI? Is a thermostat intelligent? Is an LLM "thinking"? By the end of this phase, you will have a crystal-clear, mathematically grounded, yet wonderfully intuitive understanding of what Artificial Intelligence truly is, where it came from, and how all of modern machine learning boils down to four simple ingredients.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the time you finish this phase, you will be able to:

1. **Distinguish Computation from Intelligence:** Articulate precisely why a pocket calculator doing 10-digit multiplication is *not* intelligent, but a system that adapts to unfamiliar inputs is.
2. **Deconstruct the Agent Loop:** Formulate any intelligent entity as an agent interacting with an environment through observations, internal state, actions, and rewards ($S, A, P, R, \gamma$).
3. **Trace the AI Timeline & Paradigm Shifts:** Understand why the early pioneers failed with purely symbolic expert systems, why the "AI Winters" occurred (including Minsky and Papert's mathematical proof of single-layer perceptron limitations), and what unlocked the deep learning revolution.
4. **Master the Four Pillars of Machine Learning:** Deconstruct any modern AI system (from a simple house price predictor to a 100-billion parameter transformer) into:
   $$\text{Data} + \text{Model} + \text{Objective} + \text{Optimization}$$
5. **Derive and Step Through Optimization:** Manually compute a loss function and take analytical gradient steps to adjust parameters without touching PyTorch or NumPy.
