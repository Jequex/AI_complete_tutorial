Topic 17.2: Causal Self-Attention & Pre-LN Transformer Blocks

Enforcing the Arrow of Time with Causal Masks and Residual Highways

Executive Summary

Generative autoregressive language models predict next tokens conditioning solely on past context: $$P(x_1, \dots, x_T) = \prod_{t=1}^T P(x_t \mid x_1, \dots, x_{t-1})$$ If token $t$ were allowed to attend to token $t+1$, the model would cheat during training. Causal Masking enforces this temporal causality by setting upper-triangular attention logits to $-\infty$, ensuring zero attention weight to the future. Surrounding attention is the Pre-LayerNorm Transformer Block, pairing causal attention with a $4\times$ expanded MLP and a direct residual stream.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Time-Travel Blocking Shield & The Assembly Line

Imagine friends sitting around a campfire taking turns telling a story word-by-word:

  • The Time-Travel Blocking Shield (Causal Mask): When Person 2 is trying to guess what word comes next, we put a one-way mirrored visor on them. They can look backward at what was already said, but anyone standing in the future is covered in pitch-black shadow ($-\infty$). They are physically blocked from cheating!
  • The Conveyor Belt Highway (Residual Stream): Imagine an assembly line conveyor belt. The bare car chassis rides on the belt ($\mathbf{x}$). At Station 1 (Attention), robot arms look at what's been added, build a side mirror, and bolt it on ($\mathbf{x} + \text{Attention}(\text{LN}(\mathbf{x}))$). At Station 2 (The MLP Engine Room), a $4\times$ wider workstation builds an engine piece and bolts it on ($\mathbf{x} + \text{MLP}(\text{LN}(\mathbf{x}))$). The original chassis is never wiped out; every station just contributes helpful additions!

Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Domain Description
$\mathbf{X}$$\mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times d_{\text{model}}}$Hidden activation tensor along the residual stream.
$M$$\mathbb{R}^{T \times T}$Causal mask: $M_{ij} = 0$ for $j \le i$, and $-\infty$ for $j > i$.
$A$$\mathbb{R}^{T \times T}$Softmax attention weight matrix with lower-triangular values.
$\text{LN}(\mathbf{x})$$\mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}^D$Layer Normalization: $\frac{\mathbf{x} - \mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \epsilon}} \odot \boldsymbol{\gamma} + \boldsymbol{\beta}$.
$\text{GELU}(x)$$\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$Gaussian Error Linear Unit: $x \, P(Z \le x), \; Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$.
$d_{\text{ff}}$$\mathbb{N}$MLP intermediate dimension ($4 \times d_{\text{model}}$).

Causal Masking Mechanics

Given raw scaled dot-product attention scores $S_{ij} = \frac{\mathbf{q}_i \cdot \mathbf{k}_j}{\sqrt{d_k}}$, we add the causal mask $M$ before softmax:

$$A_{ij} = \text{softmax}_j\left( S_{ij} + M_{ij} \right) = \frac{\exp(S_{ij} + M_{ij})}{\sum_{m=1}^T \exp(S_{im} + M_{im})}$$

Since $\exp(-\infty) = 0$, every future position $j > i$ receives an attention weight of exactly $0.0$.

Pre-LayerNorm Block Equations

$$\mathbf{x}^{(1)} = \mathbf{x} + \text{CausalSelfAttention}(\text{LN}_1(\mathbf{x}))$$ $$\mathbf{x}_{\text{out}} = \mathbf{x}^{(1)} + \text{MLP}(\text{LN}_2(\mathbf{x}^{(1)}))$$ 
Position-Wise Feed-Forward Network:
MLP(z) = GELU(z * W_1 + b_1) * W_2 + b_2
where W_1: [d_model -> 4*d_model],  W_2: [4*d_model -> d_model]
