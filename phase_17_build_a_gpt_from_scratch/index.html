Phase 17

Building a GPT From Scratch

From Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) subword tokenization and causal attention masks to full Transformer assembly, weight tying, and autoregressive KV-cached generation in pure Python.

01. Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) Tokenizer

Tokenization taxonomy (character vs word vs subword), the Sennrich et al. (2016) BPE algorithm, frequency counting, greedy pair merging, merge rule recording, UTF-8 byte fallbacks guaranteeing zero out-of-vocabulary (<UNK>) tokens, and handling <|endoftext|> special tokens.

📖 Lecture Document ✏️ Exercises & Solutions Markdown Source

02. Causal Self-Attention & Pre-LN Transformer Blocks

Temporal causality in autoregressive models, upper-triangular negative infinity masks ($M_{ij} = -\infty$ for $j > i$), Pre-LayerNorm architecture, residual stream gradient highways, and position-wise Feed-Forward Networks (MLP) with $4\times$ expansion and GELU activations.

📖 Lecture Document ✏️ Exercises & Solutions Markdown Source

03. Full GPT Architecture & Weight Tying

End-to-end model assembly (Token embeddings $W_{\text{token}}$, Learned position embeddings $W_{\text{pos}}$, $N$ Transformer blocks, final LayerNorm, and LM Head). Weight Tying (Press & Wolf, 2017) saving 24% of parameters, and closed-form parameter formulas for GPT-2 Small (124M), Medium (355M), Large (774M), and XL (1.5B).

📖 Lecture Document ✏️ Exercises & Solutions Markdown Source

04. Autoregressive Generation & KV-Caching

The autoregressive token-by-token generation loop, decoding policies (Greedy Search, Temperature Scaling, Top-$k$ filtering, Top-$p$ Nucleus sampling), and the Key-Value (KV) Cache mechanism reducing per-token generation complexity from $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}(T)$.
