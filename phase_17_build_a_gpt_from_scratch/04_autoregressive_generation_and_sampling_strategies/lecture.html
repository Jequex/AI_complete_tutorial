Topic 17.4: Autoregressive Generation & KV-Caching

Decoding Policies, Nucleus Sampling & Accelerating LLM Inference via Key-Value Caching

Executive Summary

Inference in GPT models is an iterative feedback loop: given context $\mathbf{x}_{1:T}$, the model predicts next-token probabilities $P(x_{T+1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1:T})$, a sampling policy chooses the new token, and it is appended to the context. Naive re-evaluation scales with quadratic compute $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$. The Key-Value (KV) Cache caches historical key and value tensors, reducing per-token generation complexity to $\mathcal{O}(T)$ and dramatically accelerating inference throughput.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Storyteller's Magic Dice & The Memory Notebook

Imagine a master fantasy storyteller telling an improvised bedtime adventure:

  • The Magic Dice (Sampling Strategies):
    • Greedy Search: The boring robot always picks the single most common word, quickly looping into repetitive phrases.
    • Temperature Dial: Low temperature ($\tau = 0.2$) produces safe, predictable facts. High temperature ($\tau = 2.0$) rolls a 100-sided die, generating wild, chaotic poetry!
    • Top-$p$ Nucleus Sampling: The clever storyteller looks only at the top sensible words until their probabilities reach 90% ($p=0.9$), discarding silly, nonsensical outliers.
  • The Memory Notebook (The KV-Cache):
    • The Forgetful Storyteller (No Cache): Before writing sentence 100, the author re-reads all 99 previous chapters word-for-word! It takes forever.
    • The Genius with a Notebook (KV-Cache): The author jots down key points in a pocket notebook. For sentence 100, they check only the notebook, write the sentence, add one note, and keep moving!

Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Domain Description
$\mathbf{z}$$\mathbb{R}^V$Unnormalized logit scores from the output projection head.
$\tau$$\mathbb{R}^+$Sampling temperature ($\tau > 0$).
$k$$\mathbb{N}$Top-$k$ filtering cutoff; restricts candidates to top $k$ logits.
$p$$(0, 1]$Top-$p$ nucleus cumulative probability threshold.
$K_{\text{cache}}, V_{\text{cache}}$$\mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{past}} \times d_k}$Cached key and value tensors stored across generation steps.

Decoding & Sampling Strategies

1. Temperature Softmax:

$$P(x_{T+1} = i) = \frac{\exp(z_i / \tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^V \exp(z_j / \tau)}$$

2. Top-$p$ Nucleus Filtering: Select smallest subset $\mathcal{V}^{(p)}$ where:

$$\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}^{(p)}} P(x_{T+1} = i) \ge p$$

The Key-Value (KV) Cache Mechanism

Because past keys and values do not change with new autoregressive tokens, caching them eliminates redundant projection math:

Without KV-Cache (Naive):    Total Compute ~ O(M^3) across M tokens
With KV-Cache (Accelerated): Total Compute ~ O(M^2) across M tokens (Linear O(T) per step)
