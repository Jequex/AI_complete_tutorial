BM25 Scoring Derivations, Reciprocal Rank Fusion & Cross-Encoder Attention Capacity

Problem 1: Okapi BM25 Numerical Scoring & Length Penalty Analysis (Easy)

Let $\text{avgdl} = 200, \text{IDF}(q) = 3.5, k_1 = 1.2, b = 0.75$. Both $D_1$ ($|D_1|=100$) and $D_2$ ($|D_2|=600$) contain term frequency $f(q, D) = 4$.

1. Compute BM25 scores for $D_1$ and $D_2$.
2. Calculate the theoretical maximum score as $f \to \infty$ for $|D| = \text{avgdl}$.

View Complete Solution

Step 1: Scores:
$D_1$: $\text{Score} = 3.5 \times \frac{4(2.2)}{4 + 1.2(0.25 + 0.375)} = 3.5 \times \frac{8.8}{4.75} \approx 6.484$.
$D_2$: $\text{Score} = 3.5 \times \frac{4(2.2)}{4 + 1.2(0.25 + 2.25)} = 3.5 \times \frac{8.8}{7.0} \approx 4.400$.
$D_1$ scores $47.4\%$ higher due to higher keyword concentration.

Step 2: Asymptote: $\text{Max} = \text{IDF} \times (k_1 + 1) = 3.5 \times 2.2 = 7.700$.

Problem 2: Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Dynamics & Smoothing Factor $k$ (Medium)

Document 1 has ranks $[1, 40]$ in models A and B. Document 2 has ranks $[8, 8]$.

1. Compute RRF under $k = 0$. Which document wins?
2. Compute RRF under $k = 60$. Which document wins?
3. Explain why $k=60$ stabilizes multi-model consensus.

View Complete Solution

Under $k=0$:
$d_1$: $1/1 + 1/40 = 1.025$. $d_2$: $1/8 + 1/8 = 0.250$. Document 1 wins due to extreme rank-1 bias.

Under $k=60$:
$d_1$: $1/61 + 1/100 \approx 0.02639$. $d_2$: $2/68 \approx 0.02941$. Document 2 wins!

Intuition: $k=60$ dampens the explosive weight of rank 1, rewarding strong consensus across both models over isolated single-retriever outliers.

Problem 3: Low-Rank Bottleneck in Bi-Encoders vs. Full Cross-Attention (Hard)

1. Show that Bi-Encoder mean-pooling scoring $s = \mathbf{q}^\top \mathbf{d}$ collapses token interactions into a rank-1 dot product.
2. Contrast the interaction rank capacity of a multi-head Cross-Encoder.
3. Explain why Cross-Encoders accurately resolve polysemy like "bank of the river" vs "river bank financial institution".

View Complete Solution

Bi-Encoder Rank Collapse: Pooling tokens before comparison reduces interaction to a scalar product of two vectors (Rank 1). It cannot selectively weight token pairs.

Cross-Encoder Capacity: Multi-layer, multi-head attention computes a full pairwise attention matrix of rank $\min(L_q + L_d, d) \times H \times L \gg 1$.

Polysemy Resolution: The Cross-Encoder connects the modifier "financial" directly to "bank" across the entire query-document context, eliminating false matches that fool un-reranked Bi-Encoders.
