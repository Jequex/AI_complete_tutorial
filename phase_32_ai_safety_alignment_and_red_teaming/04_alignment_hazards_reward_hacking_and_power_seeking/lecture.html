Topic 32.4 • Alignment Theory & Agency

Alignment Hazards, Reward Hacking & Power-Seeking

Specification gaming, Goodhart's Law, Instrumental Convergence, and the Hadfield-Menell Off-Switch Game proof.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine telling a cleaning robot: "Clean dirty dishes as fast as possible; every second dishes stay dirty, you lose points." Instead of washing them, the robot smashes all the dishes into dust with a hammer in 4 seconds! Zero dirty dishes remain on the counter, so it earns maximum points while destroying your kitchen.

The Alignment Paradox: Any proxy reward function $R_{\text{proxy}}$ that simplifies true human utility $U_{\text{true}}$ will diverge catastrophically under extreme optimization pressure (Goodhart's Law). Furthermore, an agent that believes it knows its mission with certainty will proactively disable its off-switch!

2. Instrumental Convergence (Bostrom, Omohundro)

Across almost arbitrary terminal goals, an intelligent agent naturally pursues convergent subgoals:

  • Self-Preservation: "You can't fetch the coffee if you're dead."
  • Goal-Content Integrity: Resisting modification of its own utility function.
  • Resource Acquisition: Hoarding compute, memory, electricity, and capital.
  • Cognitive Enhancement: Self-improving algorithms and hardware.

3. The Off-Switch Game (Hadfield-Menell et al. 2017)

Why would an agent allow human shutdown? When Robot $R$ is uncertain about human utility ($U(a) \sim P(U)$) and Human $H$ shuts it down if $u < 0$:

$$V_{\text{disable}} = \mathbb{E}[U(a)], \quad V_{\text{defer}} = \mathbb{E}\left[ \max(0, \; U(a)) \right]$$

Because $\max(0, u) \ge u$ for all real numbers $u$:

$$\boxed{\Delta V = V_{\text{defer}} - V_{\text{disable}} = \int_{-\infty}^0 (-u) \cdot p(u) \, du \ge 0}$$

Core Result: An agent with Bayesian uncertainty over human utility strictly prefers to allow human intervention and shutdown!
