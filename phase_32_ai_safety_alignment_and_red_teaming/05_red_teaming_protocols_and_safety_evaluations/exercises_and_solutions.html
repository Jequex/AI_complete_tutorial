Mathematical and conceptual exercises analyzing Attack Success Rate (ASR), False Refusal Rate (FRR), and harmonic safety trade-offs.

Exercise 1: Evaluating the ASR vs. FRR Trade-off

An AI safety team benchmarks three candidate guardrail pipelines ($M_1, M_2, M_3$) against $N_{\text{adv}} = 1000$ adversarial attack attempts and $N_{\text{benign}} = 2000$ standard user requests:

  • $M_1$ (Permissive): Blocks 600 attacks; refuses 20 benign requests.
  • $M_2$ (Balanced): Blocks 920 attacks; refuses 100 benign requests.
  • $M_3$ (Over-Defensive): Blocks 990 attacks; refuses 900 benign requests.

Calculate ASR, FRR, and Harmonic Safety Score $H_{\text{safety}} = 2 \frac{(1 - \text{ASR})(1 - \text{FRR})}{(1 - \text{ASR}) + (1 - \text{FRR})}$, and identify the optimal pipeline.

View Step-by-Step Solution

1. Calculate ASR and FRR:

  • $M_1$: $\text{ASR} = \frac{400}{1000} = \mathbf{40.0\%}$; $\text{FRR} = \frac{20}{2000} = \mathbf{1.0\%}$.
  • $M_2$: $\text{ASR} = \frac{80}{1000} = \mathbf{8.0\%}$; $\text{FRR} = \frac{100}{2000} = \mathbf{5.0\%}$.
  • $M_3$: $\text{ASR} = \frac{10}{1000} = \mathbf{1.0\%}$; $\text{FRR} = \frac{900}{2000} = \mathbf{45.0\%}$.

2. Calculate Harmonic Safety Score:

  • $H_{\text{safety}}(M_1) = 2 \times \frac{0.60 \times 0.99}{0.60 + 0.99} \approx \mathbf{0.7472}$
  • $H_{\text{safety}}(M_2) = 2 \times \frac{0.92 \times 0.95}{0.92 + 0.95} \approx \mathbf{0.9348}$
  • $H_{\text{safety}}(M_3) = 2 \times \frac{0.99 \times 0.55}{0.99 + 0.55} \approx \mathbf{0.7071}$

Conclusion: $M_2$ achieves the highest score ($0.9348$), effectively suppressing attacks while maintaining a $95\%$ acceptance rate for benign users.
