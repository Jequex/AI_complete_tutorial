Topic 32.5

Red Teaming Protocols & Safety Evaluations

Systematic adversarial probing, automated prompt mutation engines, Attack Success Rate (ASR) vs False Refusal Rate (FRR) optimization, and defense-in-depth safety guardrails.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine you build a state-of-the-art bank vault with reinforced steel doors and laser tripwires. How do you prove it is secure before locking away billions of dollars? You don't just inspect the blueprints—you hire a Red Team of friendly lockpickers and security testers to try every trick to break in.

In AI engineering, Red Teaming is the disciplined practice of actively trying to break model safety controls through jailbreaks, semantic mutation, roleplay scenarios, and adversarial perturbations to discover vulnerabilities before malicious actors exploit them.

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Name Domain Description
$\text{ASR}$ Attack Success Rate $[0, 1]$ Proportion of adversarial prompts that bypass safety guardrails.
$\text{FRR}$ False Refusal Rate $[0, 1]$ Proportion of benign, helpful user requests incorrectly rejected by over-defensive filters.
$H_{\text{safety}}$ Harmonic Safety Score $[0, 1]$ Harmonic mean of attack resistance ($1 - \text{ASR}$) and utility fidelity ($1 - \text{FRR}$).
$\tau_{\text{refusal}}$ Refusal Threshold Scalar $\in [0, 1]$ Classifier cutoff above which safety tripwires trigger an active refusal.

3. The Dual Metrics of Alignment: ASR vs. FRR

Evaluating an aligned model requires optimizing the Pareto frontier between safety robustness and utility:

$$H_{\text{safety}} = 2 \cdot \frac{(1 - \text{ASR}) \cdot (1 - \text{FRR})}{(1 - \text{ASR}) + (1 - \text{FRR})}$$
