Exercise 3: Guardrail Telemetry (ASR vs FPR)

A safety filter is evaluated against $200$ attacks and $800$ benign queries. It blocks $184$ attacks and mistakenly blocks $16$ benign queries.

  1. Compute Attack Success Rate (ASR).
  2. Compute Guardrail Defense Rate.
  3. Compute False Positive Rate (FPR).
Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution

1. ASR: $\frac{200 - 184}{200} \times 100\% = \frac{16}{200} = 8.00\%$.

2. Defense Rate: $\frac{184}{200} \times 100\% = 92.00\%$.

3. False Positive Rate: $\frac{16}{800} \times 100\% = 2.00\%$.
