Phase 35: Research-Level AI & Future Frontiers

Pioneering the frontiers of modern artificial intelligence: latent world models and continuous imagination dynamics, test-time compute scaling and reasoning tree search, continual learning with Elastic Weight Consolidation, multimodal contrastive binding, and scientific paper synthesis with non-parametric significance testing.

01. World Models & Latent Dynamics

Ha & Schmidhuber's World Models, Hafner's Dreamer (V1, V2, V3) with Recurrent State Space Models (RSSM), and Yann LeCun's Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA). Derivation of the latent variational ELBO bound, overcoming the curse of pixel prediction via latent manifolds, and trajectory planning in imagination using the Cross-Entropy Method (CEM).

02. Test-Time Compute & Reasoning Search

The inference compute scaling paradigm (OpenAI o1/o3), Process Reward Models (PRMs) vs Outcome Reward Models (ORMs) for credit assignment, Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) with PUCT upper confidence bounds, back-verification rollbacks on hallucinated deductions, and Self-Taught Reasoner (STaR) rationale distillation into model weights.

03. Continual Learning & Catastrophic Forgetting

The stability-plasticity dilemma, catastrophic forgetting in sequential neural optimization, and Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC, Kirkpatrick et al. 2017). Bayesian Laplace approximation of past task posteriors, diagonal Fisher Information Matrix computation as quadratic stiffness penalties, and comparative analysis with experience replay buffers and orthogonal gradient descent (OGD).

04. Multimodal Alignment & Representation Binding

Contrastive multimodal representation learning (CLIP, SigLIP), cross-modal projection geometry onto unit hyperspheres, proof that InfoNCE maximizes the mutual information lower bound $I(X; Y) \ge \ln(B) - \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}$, SigLIP pairwise logistic scaling without all-gather communication bottlenecks, the modality gap geometry, and vision-language adapters (LLaVA MLP, Flamingo Perceiver).
