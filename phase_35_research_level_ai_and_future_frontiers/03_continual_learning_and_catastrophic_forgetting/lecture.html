TOPIC 35.3

Continual Learning & Catastrophic Forgetting

Overcoming the stability-plasticity dilemma with Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC), Fisher Information geometry, experience replay buffers, and orthogonal gradient projection.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine you spend three years learning to play the acoustic guitar until you know every chord shape by heart. Then you decide to learn the piano. You practice piano for two months, but when you pick up your guitar again, your brain has completely forgotten how to make a single chord!

Humans don't work like this: when you learn to ride a bike, you don't forget how to swim. Your brain preserves the critical neural pathways for swimming while using flexible spare neurons for the bicycle.

Standard neural networks suffer from Catastrophic Forgetting: when trained sequentially on Task A and then Task B, naive gradient descent overwrites the weights essential for Task A. Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) calculates the Fisher Information Matrix, turning vital weights into stiff elastic springs so they cannot be disrupted when learning Task B.

3. The Bayesian Formulation of EWC

EWC approximates the posterior probability $P(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \mathcal{D}_A, \mathcal{D}_B)$ using a second-order Laplace approximation centered at the Task A mode $\boldsymbol{\theta}_A^*$:

$$\ln P(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \mathcal{D}_A, \mathcal{D}_B) = \ln P(\mathcal{D}_B \mid \boldsymbol{\theta}) + \ln P(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \mathcal{D}_A) - \ln P(\mathcal{D}_B)$$

Replacing the intractable prior $\ln P(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \mathcal{D}_A)$ with its Taylor expansion yields the diagonal EWC optimization loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{EWC}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \mathcal{L}_B(\boldsymbol{\theta}) + \sum_{i=1}^P \frac{\lambda}{2} F_{A, i} \left(\theta_i - \theta_{A, i}^*\right)^2$$

4. Parameter Dynamics: Stiff vs. Flexible Weights

The gradient update for each parameter during Task B training is:

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{EWC}}}{\partial \theta_i} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_B}{\partial \theta_i} + \lambda F_{A, i} \left(\theta_i - \theta_{A, i}^*\right)$$
  • Critical Weights ($F_{A, i} \gg 0$): The restoring spring force $\lambda F_{A, i} (\theta_i - \theta_{A, i}^*)$ dominates, anchoring the weight in place.
  • Redundant Weights ($F_{A, i} \approx 0$): The restoring force is negligible, allowing the weight to freely adapt to solve Task B.

5. Continual Learning Paradigms Compared

Paradigm Representative Method Key Mechanism
Regularization EWC, Synaptic Intelligence Quadratic Fisher penalty anchoring sensitive weights. Zero raw replay memory required.
Experience Replay GEM, A-GEM, DER++ Rehearsing a small stored buffer of past task exemplars alongside new batches.
Gradient Projection OGD (Orthogonal Gradient Descent) Projecting Task B updates onto the orthogonal null-space of Task A gradients.
Architecture Isolation PackNet, Progressive Networks Freezing subnetworks for past tasks and dynamically routing new capacity.
