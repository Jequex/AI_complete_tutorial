Topic 25.5: Flow Matching and Rectified Flows

From curved stochastic diffusion to deterministic straight-line Neural ODEs and few-step Euler generation.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: Mountain Switchbacks vs. The Bullet Train

Continuous generative models can take two very different paths:

  • Diffusion (Curved Switchbacks): Reversing random Brownian motion is like driving down a winding mountain pass with 1,000 hairpin turns. If your step is too big, you plunge off the cliff into noise. You need 50 to 100 tiny cautious steps.
  • Rectified Flows (Direct Bullet Train): Flow matching drills a laser-straight tunnel straight through the mountain: $x_t = (1 - t)x_0 + tx_1$. Because the path has zero curvature, you can travel the entire distance in 1 to 4 big Euler steps!

1. Continuous Normalizing Flows (CNFs) & The Continuity Equation

A deterministic trajectory $x_t \in \mathbb{R}^D$ is governed by the Neural ODE:

$$\frac{dx_t}{dt} = v_\theta(x_t, t), \quad t \in [0, 1]$$

The time-dependent probability density path $p_t(x)$ satisfies the Continuity Equation:

$$\frac{\partial p_t(x)}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot \left( p_t(x) v_\theta(x, t) \right) = 0$$

2. Conditional Flow Matching (CFM) Theorem

Minimizing the intractable marginal Flow Matching loss is identically equal to minimizing the Conditional Flow Matching objective:

$$L_{CFM}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{t \sim \mathcal{U}[0, 1], x_0 \sim p_0, x_1 \sim p_1} \left[ \left\| v_\theta(x_t, t) - u_t(x_t \mid x_0, x_1) \right\|^2 \right]$$ $$\nabla_\theta L_{CFM}(\theta) \equiv \nabla_\theta L_{FM}(\theta)$$

3. Optimal Transport & Rectified Flows

Using straight-line interpolation between base Gaussian noise $x_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \mathbf{I})$ and data $x_1 \sim p_{\text{data}}$:

$$x_t = (1 - t) x_0 + t x_1 \implies u_t(x_t \mid x_0, x_1) = x_1 - x_0$$
Zero-Curvature Advantage: Because target velocity is constant along the line, the trajectory acceleration $\frac{d^2 x_t}{dt^2} = 0$. Numerical Euler integration ($x_{t+\Delta t} = x_t + \Delta t \cdot v_\theta$) incurs virtually zero truncation error, enabling state-of-the-art generative modeling in 1 to 4 steps!
