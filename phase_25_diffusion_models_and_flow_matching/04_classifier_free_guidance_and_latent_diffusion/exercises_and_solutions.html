Bayes' score separation, guidance vector extrapolation, and attention speedup derivations.

Problem 1: Bayes' Rule Score Separation Easy

Derive from Bayes' rule $p(x_t \mid c) = \frac{p(x_t)p(c \mid x_t)}{p(c)}$ that:

$$\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t \mid c) = \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t) + \nabla_{x_t} \log p(c \mid x_t)$$

Explaining why $\nabla_{x_t}\log p(c) \equiv \mathbf{0}$.

View Complete Solution

Taking the logarithm gives $\log p(x_t \mid c) = \log p(x_t) + \log p(c \mid x_t) - \log p(c)$.

Differentiating with respect to $x_t$, the prior $p(c)$ is a constant with respect to data vector $x_t$. Hence $\nabla_{x_t}\log p(c) = \mathbf{0}$.

Problem 2: Latent Attention Complexity Reduction Medium

For a $1024 \times 1024$ image, compute the quadratic self-attention speedup gained by shifting from pixel space to an LDM latent space with downsampling factor $f = 8$.

View Complete Solution
  • Tokens in pixel space: $N_{\text{pixel}} = 1024 \times 1024 = 1,048,576$.
  • Tokens in latent space: $N_{\text{latent}} = 128 \times 128 = 16,384$.
  • Attention complexity: $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$.
$$\text{Speedup} = \left(\frac{1,048,576}{16,384}\right)^2 = (64)^2 = 4,096\times$$

Self-attention is 4,096 times faster in latent space!

Problem 3: Mode Sharpening under CFG Hard

Show that as guidance scale $s \to \infty$, the guided distribution $\tilde{p}_s(x \mid c) \propto p(x)^{1 - s} p(x \mid c)^s$ collapses its variance to zero, proving the fundamental trade-off between fidelity and sample diversity.

View Complete Solution

For Gaussian prior $p(x) = \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_0^2)$ and likelihood $p(c \mid x) = \mathcal{N}(c; x, \sigma_L^2)$, the guided variance is:

$$\sigma_s^2 = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{\sigma_0^2} + \frac{s}{\sigma_L^2}} = \frac{\sigma_0^2 \sigma_L^2}{\sigma_L^2 + s \sigma_0^2}$$

Taking the limit as $s \to \infty$:

$$\lim_{s \to \infty} \sigma_s^2 = 0 \implies \lim_{s \to \infty} \tilde{p}_s(x \mid c) = \delta(x - c)$$

High guidance scales force absolute fidelity to condition $c$, at the expense of collapsing sample diversity to zero.
