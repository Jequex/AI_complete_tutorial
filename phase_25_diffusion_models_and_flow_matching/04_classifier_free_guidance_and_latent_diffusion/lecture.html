Topic 25.4: Classifier-Free Guidance and Latent Diffusion Models

From classifier guidance to CFG vector extrapolation and high-efficiency latent space diffusion.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Megaphone & The Flash Drive

Two essential components power modern controlled diffusion:

  • The Megaphone (CFG): When a director whispers "draw a dragon" ($s=1$), the artist draws a mountain and hides a tiny dragon. When the director yells through a megaphone ($s=5$), the artist fills the canvas with flaming scales! Guidance scale $s$ controls prompt obedience.
  • The Compact Flash Drive (Latent Diffusion): Storing raw IMAX film at atom-level pixel detail crashes computers. Compressing the footage into a compact latent flash drive ($48\times$ smaller) lets artists paint special effects rapidly before unzipping back to 4K resolution!

1. The Mathematics of Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG)

Applying Bayes' rule to conditional score $\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t \mid c)$:

$$\nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t \mid c) = \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t) + \nabla_{x_t} \log p(c \mid x_t)$$

Notice that the classifier score is:

$$\nabla_{x_t} \log p(c \mid x_t) = \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t \mid c) - \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t)$$

Substituting into the guided score with scaling parameter $s$:

$$\nabla_{x_t} \log \tilde{p}(x_t \mid c) = \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t) + s \left( \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t \mid c) - \nabla_{x_t} \log p(x_t) \right)$$

In noise-prediction space:

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\epsilon}}_\theta(x_t, c) = \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, \emptyset) + s \cdot \left( \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, c) - \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, \emptyset) \right)$$
Joint Training: The network is trained with condition dropout (replacing $c$ with $\emptyset$ with probability $p \approx 0.20$), learning both conditional and unconditional distributions simultaneously without requiring a separate noisy classifier!

2. Latent Diffusion Models (LDM / Stable Diffusion)

Rather than diffusing across $512 \times 512 \times 3 = 786,432$ pixels, LDM uses a two-stage approach:

  1. Pretrained VAE: Maps pixel image $x$ to compact latent $z = \mathcal{E}(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times 64 \times 4}$ (a $48\times$ reduction in dimensionality).
  2. Latent Diffusion: Denoising occurs exclusively in $\mathcal{Z}$, yielding a $4096\times$ theoretical reduction in quadratic self-attention FLOPs!
  3. Cross-Attention Conditioning: Features query text tokens via multi-head attention: $\text{softmax}(QK^\top / \sqrt{d})V$.
