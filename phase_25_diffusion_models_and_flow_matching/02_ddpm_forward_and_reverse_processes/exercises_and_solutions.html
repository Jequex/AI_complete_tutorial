Closed-form marginal proofs, Bayesian posterior derivations, and Tweedie's formula connections.

Problem 1: Closed-Form Marginal Calculation Easy

For a 3-step schedule $\beta_1 = 0.10, \beta_2 = 0.20, \beta_3 = 0.30$ and $x_0 = 5.0$:

  1. Compute $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ and $\bar{\alpha}_1, \bar{\alpha}_2, \bar{\alpha}_3$.
  2. Write down the marginal Gaussian $q(x_3 \mid x_0)$.
  3. Calculate $x_3$ given injected perturbation $\epsilon = -1.2$.
View Complete Solution

Step 1: Retention factors:

$$\alpha_1 = 0.90 \implies \bar{\alpha}_1 = 0.900$$ $$\alpha_2 = 0.80 \implies \bar{\alpha}_2 = 0.720$$ $$\alpha_3 = 0.70 \implies \bar{\alpha}_3 = 0.504$$

Step 2: Distribution:

$$q(x_3 \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}\left(\sqrt{0.504} \times 5.0, 1 - 0.504\right) = \mathcal{N}(3.5496, 0.496)$$

Step 3: Sampling:

$$x_3 = 3.5496 + \sqrt{0.496} \times (-1.2) = 3.5496 - 0.8451 = 2.7045$$

Problem 2: Posterior Mean Completing the Square Medium

Derive algebraically how the linear cross-term of $x_{t-1}$ in Bayes' rule combines with $\tilde{\beta}_t = \frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} \beta_t$ to give:

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_t(x_t, x_0) = \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}(1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1})}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} x_t + \frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}\beta_t}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} x_0$$
View Complete Solution

In standard Gaussian expansion $-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}(x - \mu)^2 = -\frac{x^2}{2\sigma^2} + \frac{\mu}{\sigma^2}x - \frac{\mu^2}{2\sigma^2}$:

$$\frac{\tilde{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_t}{\tilde{\beta}_t} = \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}{\beta_t} x_t + \frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_0$$

Multiplying both sides by $\tilde{\beta}_t = \frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} \beta_t$:

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_t = \left(\frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\beta_t\right)\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}{\beta_t} x_t + \left(\frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\beta_t\right)\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_0$$

Terms cancel directly to yield:

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_t = \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}(1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1})}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} x_t + \frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}\beta_t}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} x_0$$

Problem 3: Tweedie's Formula & Score Equivalence Hard

Use Tweedie's formula $\mathbb{E}[x \mid y] = y + \sigma^2 \nabla_y \log p(y)$ to prove that predicting DDPM noise $\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, t)$ is equivalent to predicting the score vector field:

$$\nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t) = -\frac{\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, t)}{\sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}}$$
View Complete Solution

Dividing $x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 + \sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\boldsymbol{\epsilon}$ by $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}$:

$$\frac{x_t}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}} = x_0 + \sqrt{\frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}{\bar{\alpha}_t}} \boldsymbol{\epsilon}$$

Applying Tweedie's formula with observation $y = \frac{x_t}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}}$ and variance $\sigma^2 = \frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}{\bar{\alpha}_t}$:

$$\hat{x}_0 = \frac{x_t}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}} + \left(\frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}{\bar{\alpha}_t}\right)\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} \nabla_{x_t}\log q(x_t) = \frac{x_t + (1 - \bar{\alpha}_t)\nabla_{x_t}\log q(x_t)}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}}$$

In DDPM, the denoised estimate is $\hat{x}_0 = \frac{x_t - \sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}}$. Equating numerators:

$$(1 - \bar{\alpha}_t)\nabla_{x_t}\log q(x_t) = -\sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta \implies \nabla_{x_t}\log q(x_t) = -\frac{\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, t)}{\sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}}$$
