Phase 25

Diffusion Models & Flow Matching

From Score-Based SDEs and DDPM to Classifier-Free Guidance and Rectified Flow Neural ODEs

Architectural Paradigm

Phase 25 explores the modern state-of-the-art in deep generative modeling: moving from intractable partition functions to continuous score vector fields, physical non-equilibrium thermodynamics, and deterministic optimal transport flow matching. Every experiment across this phase is engineered from first principles in standard Python 3 with zero external dependencies.

01. Score-Based Models & Langevin Dynamics

The intractable partition function $Z_\theta = \int e^{-E_\theta(x)} dx$ and why the Stein score $s_\theta(x) = \nabla_x \log p(x) = -\nabla_x E_\theta(x)$ eliminates it identically. Hyvärinen's (2005) Implicit Score Matching derivation via integration by parts, Vincent's (2011) Denoising Score Matching, manifold hypothesis failure modes in data voids, and Song & Ermon's (2019) Annealed Langevin Dynamics.

02. DDPM Forward & Reverse Processes

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015, Ho et al. 2020). The forward Markov chain $q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{1 - \beta_t}x_{t-1}, \beta_t \mathbf{I})$, mathematical induction deriving the closed-form shortcut $q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0, (1 - \bar{\alpha}_t)\mathbf{I})$, and the complete Bayesian derivation of the true posterior $q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0)$ by completing the square in Gaussian exponents.

03. Noise Schedules & Denoising U-Net Architectures

The mathematical flaw of linear variance schedules, the Cosine Noise Schedule (Nichol & Dhariwal 2021) with offset $s=0.008$, complete derivation of the Variational Lower Bound (ELBO) decomposition into Gaussian KL divergences, Jonathan Ho's simplified variational objective $L_{\text{simple}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\|\boldsymbol{\epsilon} - \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(x_t, t)\|^2]$, and U-Net structural motifs (sinusoidal harmonic time embeddings, residual blocks, and multi-scale skip connections).

04. Classifier-Free Guidance & Latent Diffusion (LDM)

Conditional generation $p(x \mid c)$, Classifier Guidance $\nabla \log p(x_t \mid c) = \nabla \log p(x_t) + \nabla \log p(c \mid x_t)$, Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG; Ho & Salimans 2021) joint training with condition dropout ($p_{\text{uncond}} \approx 0.2$), guidance extrapolation $\tilde{\boldsymbol{\epsilon}} = \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\emptyset + s(\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_c - \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\emptyset)$, mode sharpening versus diversity trade-offs, and Latent Diffusion Models (Stable Diffusion; Rombach et al. 2022) shifting diffusion into the perceptual latent space of a pretrained VAE for a $48\times$ dimensionality reduction and a $4096\times$ self-attention acceleration.

05. Flow Matching & Rectified Flows

Continuous Normalizing Flows (CNFs) and Neural ODEs $\frac{dx_t}{dt} = v_\theta(x_t, t)$, probability mass conservation via the Continuity Equation $\frac{\partial p_t}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (p_t v) = 0$, the Flow Matching objective (Lipman et al. 2022), Conditional Flow Matching with optimal transport interpolation $x_t = (1 - t)x_0 + tx_1$ yielding constant target velocity $u_t = x_1 - x_0$, and Rectified Flows (Liu et al. 2022) with zero-curvature trajectories enabling high-fidelity data generation in 1 to 4 numerical Euler steps.
