Topic 27.5

Multi-Agent Orchestration & Communication Protocols

Topologies, FIPA-ACL structured message envelopes, adversarial debate mechanics, and Condorcet consensus guarantees.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine an emergency operating room during heart surgery. If one doctor tried to simultaneously monitor the heartbeat, operate the heart-lung pump, cut with the scalpel, and hand themselves sterile tools, the patient would be in grave danger.

Instead, a hospital relies on a specialized team:

  • Lead Surgeon: Focuses purely on delicate surgical incisions (Specialized Executor).
  • Anesthesiologist: Audits vital signs and warns if oxygen or pressure drops (The Critic).
  • Scrub Nurse: Hands the exact tools needed (Tool Specialist).
  • Chief of Surgery: Resolves medical disagreements and ratifies the final plan (The Judge).

They use strict verbal communication protocols ("Requesting 10mg Epinephrine" → "Confirming 10mg drawn" → "Administer now"). Multi-agent AI systems mirror this exact architecture.

4. Condorcet's Jury Theorem & Error Suppression

Why does multi-agent debate outperform a single agent? If each agent has an independent probability $p > 0.5$ of finding or verifying a correct step, the majority vote accuracy across $N$ debating agents is:

$$\boxed{P_N = \sum_{k=\frac{N+1}{2}}^N \binom{N}{k} p^k (1 - p)^{N - k}}$$

As $N \to \infty$, $P_N \to 1.0$. Even modest individual models, when configured as specialized Proposers and Critics, amplify ensemble accuracy toward near-certainty.
