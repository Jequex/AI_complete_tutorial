Topic 33.3

Attention Head Mechanisms & Induction Heads

Deconstructing Transformer Circuits: QK-circuits, OV-circuits, and the two-layer induction engine powering in-context learning.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine a card game where the dealer lays down: [Red Circle] [Blue Triangle] [Green Square] ... [Red Circle]. What comes next? Your brain immediately responds: "Blue Triangle!"

Transformers accomplish this with a 2-head team:

  • Player 1 (Layer 0 Previous-Token Head): Every token writes down a note: "The token right before me was X."
  • Player 2 (Layer 1 Induction Head): When token X appears again, Player 2 searches the notes for whoever wrote "Previous was X", attends to that token, and copies it to the output!
The Breakthrough: In-context learning $[A][B] \dots [A] \to [B]$ is not statistical magic; it is an exact mechanical 2-layer circuit!

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Entity Domain Role in Transformer Circuits
$W_{QK} = W_Q^\top W_K$ QK-Circuit Matrix $\mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ Bilinear operator computing attention logits: $\alpha_{i, j} = \mathbf{x}_i^\top W_{QK} \mathbf{x}_j / \sqrt{d_k}$.
$W_{OV} = W_O W_V$ OV-Circuit Matrix $\mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ Linear operator defining what representation is copied to the destination: $\sum_j A_{i, j} W_{OV} \mathbf{x}_j$.
$H_{0, \text{prev}}$ Previous-Token Head Layer 0 Copies token identity at $j-1$ into the residual stream at position $j$.
$H_{1, \text{ind}}$ Induction Head Layer 1 Matches current token $i$ with previous-token information to attend to $j$ and predict the subsequent token.
$W_U W_{OV} W_E$ End-to-End Direct Path $\mathbb{R}^{V \times V}$ Token-to-token projection matrix; positive eigenvalues indicate direct copying behavior.

3. The QK and OV Mathematical Decomposition

Anthropic's Transformer Circuits framework divides attention heads into two independent circuits:

$$\text{Attention Logits: } \alpha_{i, j} = \frac{\mathbf{x}_i^\top W_{QK} \mathbf{x}_j}{\sqrt{d_{\text{head}}}}, \quad A_{i, :} = \text{softmax}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i, :})$$ $$\text{Residual Stream Contribution: } \Delta \mathbf{x}_i = \sum_{j} A_{i, j} W_{OV} \mathbf{x}_j$$

Notice that $W_{QK}$ controls where information is routed, while $W_{OV}$ controls what information is transported.

4. Circuit Composition: Why Two Layers Are Required

The 1-Layer Impossibility Theorem

A single-layer model cannot implement $[A][B] \dots [A] \to [B]$. If it attends to $[A]$, it can only copy $[A]$. To predict $[B]$, it must attend to $[B]$ based on the fact that $[B]$ follows $[A]$.

Layer 0 writes the identity of token $j-1$ into position $j$. Layer 1's $QK$-circuit contains the composite interaction:

$$\alpha_{i, j} \supset [A]^\top \left( W_E^\top (W_Q^{(1)})^\top W_K^{(1)} W_{OV}^{(0)} W_E \right) [A]$$

This positive interaction causes the induction head at the second $[A]$ to attend directly to $[B]$ with near $100\%$ probability!
