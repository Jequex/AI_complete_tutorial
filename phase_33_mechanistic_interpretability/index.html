Phase 33: Mechanistic Interpretability

Reverse-engineering neural networks: residual stream geometry, causal activation patching, the Transformer Circuits framework, two-layer induction heads, Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs), and rank-one model editing (ROME).

01. Linear Probing & Representation Geometry

The residual stream as a communication bus, the Linear Representation Hypothesis (concepts as directions $\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^d$), linear probes (logistic regression & ridge classification on intermediate activations), probe accuracy vs cosine similarity, evaluating probe generalization and ruling out probe memorization using the Hewitt & Liang Selectivity control test.

02. Activation Patching & Causal Tracing

Causal intervention methodology: Clean run, Corrupted/Counterfactual run, and Patched run; Causal Tracing (Meng et al. 2022, ROME): isolating the precise layers and attention heads storing and retrieving factual knowledge; Average Treatment Effect (ATE) on logits $\Delta \text{logit} = \text{logit}(y_{\text{clean}}) - \text{logit}(y_{\text{corrupt}})$, and Interchange Intervention Training (IIT).

03. Attention Head Mechanisms & Induction Heads

The Transformer Circuits framework (Elhage et al. 2021): $QK$-circuits (determining where attention is paid, $A = \text{softmax}(x^\top W_Q^\top W_K x)$) and $OV$-circuits (determining what information is copied, $W_O W_V$); Eigenvalue analysis of $W_E^\top W_U$ (unembed-embed matrix); Two-layer Induction Heads executing in-context pattern completion $[A][B] \dots [A] \to [B]$ via previous-token head and induction head composition.

04. Polysemanticity & Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs)

The Superposition Hypothesis: Neural networks represent more semantic features than available vector dimensions ($D \gg d$) by packing almost-orthogonal features into high-dimensional space; Polysemanticity: single neurons activating for unrelated concepts; Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs, Bricken et al. 2023): overcomplete dictionary learning ($f(x) = \text{ReLU}(W_{\text{enc}} x + b_{\text{enc}})$) with $L_1$ sparsity penalty $\mathcal{L}_{\text{SAE}} = \|x - \hat{x}\|_2^2 + \lambda \sum_i |f_i(x)|$ recovering monosemantic latent features.

05. Model Steering & Weight Editing

Activation steering vectors: Representation engineering (Zou et al. 2023), computing concept steering vectors from contrastive pairs $\mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum x_{\text{positive}} - \frac{1}{M}\sum x_{\text{negative}}$, runtime activation intervention $\mathbf{h}' = \mathbf{h} + \alpha \mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}}$; Direct weight editing: Rank-One Model Editing (ROME, Meng et al. 2022) modifying MLP second-layer projection via closed-form rank-1 updates $W' = W + \Lambda k^\top$ preserving existing knowledge covariance.
