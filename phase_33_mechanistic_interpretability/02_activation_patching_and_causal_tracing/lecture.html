Topic 33.2

Activation Patching & Causal Tracing

Isolating the precise layers and attention heads storing and routing factual knowledge through causal mediation analysis.

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine a bank vault is unlocked in a 12-story building with 40 employees. Who typed the passcode? Looking at who was sitting at their desk only reveals correlations. Everyone looks busy.

To prove causal responsibility:

  1. Clean Day: Alice is on Floor 4, and the vault opens with code "PARIS".
  2. Corrupted Day: Bob replaces Alice, and the vault fails to open ("ROME").
  3. The Patch: While Bob is working, you secretly swap Alice's notebook onto the desk of Floor 4. If the vault unlocks, Floor 4 was the exact causal driver of the passcode!
The Scientific Principle: Causal Intervention replaces observational correlations with controlled surgical state swaps to isolate neural circuits.

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Entity Domain Interpretability Significance
$x_{\text{clean}}, x_{\text{corrupt}}$ Input Prompts Tokens Clean prompt (e.g., "Eiffel Tower in") vs corrupted prompt ("Colosseum in").
$\mathbf{a}_{\text{clean}}(l, t)$ Intermediate Activation $\mathbb{R}^d$ Activation state at layer $l$, token position $t$ captured during clean pass.
$\Delta L$ Logit Difference Real scalar $\text{logit}(y_{\text{clean}}) - \text{logit}(y_{\text{corrupt}})$, measuring preference for ground truth.
$\mathcal{R}(l, t)$ Normalized Restoration $[0, 1]$ Ratio of logit difference recovered when patching $(l, t)$.
$\text{ATE}$ Average Treatment Effect Real scalar Expected causal impact of activation replacement on the output distribution.

3. Normalized Logit Restoration Formula

When patching an individual component at layer $l$ and token position $t$, the recovery percentage is:

$$\boxed{\mathcal{R}(l, t) = \frac{\Delta L_{\text{patched}}(l, t) - \Delta L_{\text{corrupt}}}{\Delta L_{\text{clean}} - \Delta L_{\text{corrupt}}}}$$

where $\Delta L = \text{logit}(y_*) - \text{logit}(y_{\text{alt}})$. A value $\mathcal{R}(l, t) \approx 100\%$ indicates that the patched node acts as an indispensable causal bottleneck for retrieving the factual association.

4. The ROME Causal Tracing Architecture (Meng et al. 2022)

Two Distinct Functional Circuits

  1. Early/Mid-Layer MLPs at the Subject Token: Factual associations are retrieved from intermediate feed-forward weights acting as associative memories (key-value dictionaries).
  2. Late-Layer Attention Heads at the Final Token: Information movers read the retrieved factual concept from the subject residual stream and transport it to the final token for output projection.
