Exercise 1: Derivation of the Closed-Form ROME Rank-One Update

Derive the closed-form update $W' = W + \boldsymbol{\Lambda} (\mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*)^\top$ by minimizing $\frac{1}{2}\text{Tr}(\Delta W \mathbf{C} \Delta W^\top)$ subject to $(W + \Delta W)\mathbf{k}_* = \mathbf{v}_*$.

  1. Formulate the Lagrangian with vector multiplier $\boldsymbol{\nu}$.
  2. Prove that $\Delta W = \boldsymbol{\nu} (\mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*)^\top$ has rank at most 1.
  3. Solve for $\boldsymbol{\nu}$ in terms of target residual $\mathbf{v}_* - W\mathbf{k}_*$ and scalar $\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*$.
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. Lagrangian Function

$$\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2}\text{Tr}(\Delta W \mathbf{C} \Delta W^\top) + \boldsymbol{\nu}^\top(\mathbf{v}_* - W \mathbf{k}_* - \Delta W \mathbf{k}_*)$$

2. Stationarity & Rank-1 Structure

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \Delta W} = \Delta W \mathbf{C} - \boldsymbol{\nu} \mathbf{k}_*^\top = 0 \implies \Delta W = \boldsymbol{\nu} (\mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*)^\top$$

Being an outer product of two vectors, $\Delta W$ is algebraically a rank-one matrix.

3. Lagrange Multiplier Value

$$\Delta W \mathbf{k}_* = \boldsymbol{\nu}(\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*) = \mathbf{v}_* - W \mathbf{k}_* \implies \boldsymbol{\nu} = \frac{\mathbf{v}_* - W \mathbf{k}_*}{\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*}$$

Exercise 2: Why $\mathbf{C}^{-1}$ Prevents Catastrophic Forgetting

Compare naive rank-one update $W_{\text{naive}}' = W + \frac{\mathbf{v}_* - W\mathbf{k}_*}{\|\mathbf{k}_*\|^2} \mathbf{k}_*^\top$ with ROME's covariance-weighted update.

  1. Compute the drift on an existing memory $\mathbf{k}_j$ when $\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{k}_j = \rho > 0$ under naive updating.
  2. Explain why pre-multiplying by $\mathbf{C}^{-1}$ shields existing memories from drift.
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. Naive Drift

$$\|W_{\text{naive}}' \mathbf{k}_j - W \mathbf{k}_j\|_2 = \frac{|\rho|}{\|\mathbf{k}_*\|_2^2} \|\mathbf{v}_* - W \mathbf{k}_*\|_2$$

Any non-zero correlation directly perturbs key $\mathbf{k}_j$, destroying pre-existing knowledge.

2. Covariance Projection Shield

The inverse covariance $\mathbf{C}^{-1}$ penalizes directions that have high variance in existing data ($\frac{1}{\sigma_i^2} \ll 1$). The update is directed exclusively into orthogonal, unallocated null spaces where $\mathbf{k}_j^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_* \approx 0$.

Exercise 3: Steering Gain $\alpha$ & LayerNorm Saturation

Consider steering intervention $\mathbf{h}' = \mathbf{h} + \alpha \mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}}$ followed by LayerNorm $\text{LN}(\mathbf{h}') = \frac{\mathbf{h}'}{\sigma(\mathbf{h}')}$.

  1. Show what happens to prompt information $\mathbf{h}$ when $\alpha \to \infty$.
  2. Why does extreme gain collapse generation into degenerate repetition?
  3. What is the recommended empirical gain range?
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. LayerNorm Scaling Collapse

For large $\alpha$, $\sigma(\mathbf{h}') \approx \alpha \sigma(\mathbf{v})$. Thus $\text{LN}(\mathbf{h}') \approx \frac{1}{\alpha}\frac{\mathbf{h}}{\sigma(\mathbf{v})} + \frac{\mathbf{v}}{\sigma(\mathbf{v})}$. As $\alpha \to \infty$, the original prompt representation is extinguished ($\frac{1}{\alpha} \to 0$).

2. Degeneracy

The model loses awareness of context, grammar, and prompt instructions, outputting single-token loops.

3. Recommended Range

$\alpha$ should scale the steering injection to $10\% \text{--} 40\%$ of the residual stream norm ($\alpha \in [1.0, 3.5]$ for unit vectors).
