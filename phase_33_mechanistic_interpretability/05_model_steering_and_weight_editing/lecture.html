Topic 33.5

Model Steering & Weight Editing

Modulating model behavior at inference with representation engineering and surgical rank-one factual weight updates (ROME).

1. Introduction & 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine you have a robot assistant. Sometimes you want a simple mood dial: turn it to $+3$ for optimism, or $-3$ for skepticism. That is Activation Steering: adding a concept vector $\mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}}$ directly to its intermediate thoughts.

Other times, the robot has one specific mistaken fact: it thinks Seattle is the capital of the Moon. Retraining the robot costs millions of dollars. But with Rank-One Model Editing (ROME), you calculate an exact, closed-form rank-1 matrix update that rewires that single memory chip while leaving all other knowledge completely intact!

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Entity Domain Interpretability Significance
$\mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}}$ Steering Vector $\mathbb{R}^d, \|\mathbf{v}\|=1$ Concept direction derived from contrastive mean differences: $\frac{1}{N}\sum \mathbf{h}_+ - \frac{1}{M}\sum \mathbf{h}_-$.
$\alpha$ Steering Multiplier Real scalar Gain coefficient controlling intervention magnitude: $\mathbf{h}' = \mathbf{h} + \alpha \mathbf{v}_{\text{steer}}$.
$\mathbf{k}_*, \mathbf{v}_*$ Target Key-Value Pair $\mathbf{k}_* \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{in}}}, \mathbf{v}_* \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}}}$ Subject representation to edit and the new desired factual target vector.
$\mathbf{C}$ Knowledge Covariance $\mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{in}} \times d_{\text{in}}}$ $\mathbf{C} = \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{k}\mathbf{k}^\top]$, capturing the distribution of all unedited memories.
$\boldsymbol{\Lambda}$ ROME Update Vector $\mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}}}$ Closed-form rank-1 gain vector: $\boldsymbol{\Lambda} = (\mathbf{v}_* - W\mathbf{k}_*) / (\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*)$.

3. The Closed-Form ROME Update (Meng et al. 2022)

To insert target fact $W' \mathbf{k}_* = \mathbf{v}_*$ while minimizing drift on prior factual covariance $\mathbf{C} = \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{k}\mathbf{k}^\top]$:

$$\min_{W'} \text{Tr}\left( (W' - W) \mathbf{C} (W' - W)^\top \right) \quad \text{subject to} \quad W' \mathbf{k}_* = \mathbf{v}_*$$

The Optimal Rank-One Solution

$$\boxed{W' = W + \boldsymbol{\Lambda} \left( \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_* \right)^\top}$$ $$\boxed{\boldsymbol{\Lambda} = \frac{\mathbf{v}_* - W \mathbf{k}_*}{\mathbf{k}_*^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_*}}$$

This formulation guarantees $100\%$ Efficacy on the new target key ($W' \mathbf{k}_* = \mathbf{v}_*$) and Near-Zero Specificity Drift on all orthogonal memories ($\mathbf{k}_j^\top \mathbf{C}^{-1} \mathbf{k}_* = 0 \implies W' \mathbf{k}_j = W \mathbf{k}_j$).
