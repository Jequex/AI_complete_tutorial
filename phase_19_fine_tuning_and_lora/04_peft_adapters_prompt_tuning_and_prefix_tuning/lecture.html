Topic 19.4: PEFT Adapters, Prompt Tuning, and Prefix Tuning

Series & parallel bottleneck adapters, soft continuous prompting, cross-layer Key-Value prefix injection, and the architectural trade-off spectrum.

The Broad PEFT Taxonomy: Parameter-efficient adaptation spans modular sub-layer insertion (Adapters), virtual token embedding injection (Prompt Tuning), attention state concatenation (Prefix Tuning), and weight matrix reparameterization (LoRA).

1. Executive Summary & The 12-Year-Old Analogy

Before LoRA's emergence in 2021, the AI research community developed foundational alternatives: Bottleneck Adapters (Houlsby et al. 2019, He et al. 2021) and Soft Continuous Prompting (Prompt Tuning & Prefix Tuning). Understanding these methods is crucial for understanding how models can be steered without altering internal weights.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: Custom Tool Attachments for a Swiss Army Knife

Imagine you own a Swiss Army knife with 20 stainless steel tools: blades, scissors, and screwdrivers. You are entering a woodworking contest and need specialized chisels:

  • Full Fine-Tuning (Reforging the Knife): You melt the knife down and reforge it into a giant wood chisel. The original scissors and blades are permanently lost.
  • Prompt Tuning (The Instruction Sticky Note): You write "Act like a wood chisel" on a yellow sticky note and tape it to the handle. It takes up pocket room (Context Window penalty) and smaller knives might ignore it.
  • Prefix Tuning (Guide Rails on Every Joint): You snap miniature guide rails directly onto the pivot joints of every single folding blade inside the knife, steering their angles with surgical precision.
  • Series Adapters & LoRA (Clip-On Attachments): You snap an ultra-compact magnetic precision tip onto the blade. When cutting is done, you unclip it in one second.

2. Series vs. Parallel Bottleneck Adapters

Houlsby Series Adapters (2019) insert bottleneck MLPs sequentially after attention and FFN blocks:

$$\text{Adapter}(h) = h + f\left(h W_{\text{down}} + b_d\right) W_{\text{up}} + b_u$$

Because each adapter executes in sequence, it introduces sequential GPU kernel launches, increasing inference latency by $15\% - 30\%$.

Parallel Adapters (He et al. 2021) execute concurrently with base layers:

$$h_{\text{out}} = \text{BaseLayer}(x) + s \cdot \text{Adapter}(x)$$

This eliminates sequential execution bubbles on modern GPU streams.

3. Prefix Tuning vs. Prompt Tuning

  • Prompt Tuning (Lester et al. 2021): Prepends $L_p$ virtual token embeddings strictly to the input layer: $[P; E(x)]$. Extremely parameter-efficient ($0.001\%$), but consumes $L_p$ tokens of input context length.
  • Prefix Tuning (Li & Liang 2021): Prepends learnable Key and Value prefixes $P_K^{(l)}, P_V^{(l)}$ to attention at every layer $l$: $$K_{\text{full}}^{(l)} = [P_K^{(l)}; K_{\text{seq}}^{(l)}], \quad V_{\text{full}}^{(l)} = [P_V^{(l)}; V_{\text{seq}}^{(l)}]$$ This provides deep structural steering across the entire depth of the network.
