Problem 1: Manual Step-by-Step LoRA Forward Pass & Weight Merging (Easy)

Given $W_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & 2.0 \\ 3.0 & 4.0 \end{bmatrix}$, $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & -1.0 \end{bmatrix}$, $B = \begin{bmatrix} 2.0 \\ -1.0 \end{bmatrix}$, $r = 1$, $\alpha = 2.0$, and input $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 3.0 \\ 1.0 \end{bmatrix}$:

  1. Compute $\mathbf{h}_{\text{base}} = W_0 \mathbf{x}$.
  2. Compute intermediate LoRA activation $u = A \mathbf{x}$.
  3. Compute low-rank delta $\Delta \mathbf{h} = \frac{\alpha}{r} B u$.
  4. Compute total output $\mathbf{h} = \mathbf{h}_{\text{base}} + \Delta \mathbf{h}$.
  5. Compute merged weight $W_{\text{merged}} = W_0 + \frac{\alpha}{r} B A$ and verify $W_{\text{merged}} \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{h}$.
View Complete Solution

Steps 1 to 4: Dynamic Forward Pass

$$\mathbf{h}_{\text{base}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1(3) + 2(1) \\ 3(3) + 4(1) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5.0 \\ 13.0 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$u = 1(3) - 1(1) = 2.0$$ $$\frac{\alpha}{r} = 2.0 \implies \Delta \mathbf{h} = 2.0 \begin{bmatrix} 2.0 \\ -1.0 \end{bmatrix} (2.0) = \begin{bmatrix} 8.0 \\ -4.0 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$\mathbf{h} = \begin{bmatrix} 5.0 \\ 13.0 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 8.0 \\ -4.0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 13.0 \\ 9.0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Step 5: Merged Verification

$$B A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \implies \Delta W = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -4 \\ -2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$W_{\text{merged}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -4 \\ -2 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 5.0 & -2.0 \\ 1.0 & 6.0 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$W_{\text{merged}} \mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 5(3) - 2(1) \\ 1(3) + 6(1) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 13.0 \\ 9.0 \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{h} \quad \checkmark$$

Problem 2: Full-Stack Parameter Reduction in LLaMA-3-8B (Medium)

Across all 32 layers of LLaMA-3-8B, we adapt all 7 linear projections ($W_q, W_k, W_v, W_o, W_{\text{gate}}, W_{\text{up}}, W_{\text{down}}$) with rank $r = 16$.

Calculate total base parameters vs. LoRA parameters and the reduction percentage.

View Complete Solution
  • Total Base Linear Parameters: $\mathbf{6,979,321,856 \text{ parameters}}$ ($\approx 6.98\text{ B}$).
  • Total LoRA Parameters ($r=16$): $\mathbf{41,943,040 \text{ parameters}}$ ($\approx 41.94\text{ M}$).
  • Parameter Percentage: $41.94\text{M} / 6.98\text{B} = \mathbf{0.601\%}$.
  • Parameter Reduction: $99.40\%$ reduction!
