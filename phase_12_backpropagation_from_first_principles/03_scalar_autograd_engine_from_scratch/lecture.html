Topic 12.3: Scalar Autograd Engine From Scratch

Architecture Blueprint: Building a full autograd engine requires only three core abstractions:
  1. A Value container tracking numerical data, accumulated gradients, and DAG predecessors.
  2. Local differentiation closures implementing primitive operations ($+$, $\times$, $\tanh$, $\dots$).
  3. A recursive Depth-First Search (DFS) topological sort guaranteeing reverse topological backpropagation.

1. The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Magic Mathematical Echo

Imagine magic stones stacked into towers. When two stones combine to form a new stone, the new stone attaches a walkie-talkie with a recorded rule: "To tell my left parent, multiply my incoming signal by the right stone's value."

When the loss stone at the top is struck, it whispers its echo downward. Each stone multiplies the incoming sound, echoes it to its parents, and adds up vibrations if echoes arrive from multiple paths. When the sound reaches the bottom, the total vibration on each stone is exactly its derivative!

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

3. DFS Topological Sorting

To ensure that all downstream consumers of a node have contributed their gradient before that node backpropagates to its predecessors, we execute Depth-First Search post-order traversal:

def backward(self):
    topo = []
    visited = set()

    def build_topo(v):
        if v not in visited:
            visited.add(v)
            for child in v._prev:
                build_topo(child)
            topo.append(v)

    build_topo(self)
    self.grad = 1.0

    for node in reversed(topo):
        node._backward()
