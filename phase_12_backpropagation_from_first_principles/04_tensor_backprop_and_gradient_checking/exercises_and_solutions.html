Topic 12.4: Exercises & Solutions — Tensor Backpropagation & GradCheck

Problem 1: Matrix Backprop with Elementwise Nonlinearity (Medium)

For $Z = XW + b$ and $Y = \sigma(Z)$ with loss adjoint $\bar{Y}$, derive matrix formulas for $\frac{\partial L}{\partial W}$, $\frac{\partial L}{\partial X}$, and $\frac{\partial L}{\partial b}$.

Solution:

  • $\bar{Z} = \bar{Y} \odot \sigma'(Z) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times D_{\text{out}}}$
  • $\frac{\partial L}{\partial W} = X^T \bar{Z} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{in}} \times D_{\text{out}}}$
  • $\frac{\partial L}{\partial X} = \bar{Z} W^T \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times D_{\text{in}}}$
  • $\frac{\partial L}{\partial b} = \sum_{i=1}^B \bar{Z}_{i, :} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times D_{\text{out}}}$

Problem 2: Taylor Proof of Central Difference Truncation Error (Easy)

Prove that central differences achieve $\mathcal{O}(\epsilon^2)$ second-order truncation error, whereas forward differences only achieve $\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)$.

Solution:

Subtracting $f(x-\epsilon) = f(x) - \epsilon f'(x) + \frac{\epsilon^2}{2} f''(x) - \frac{\epsilon^3}{6} f'''(x)$ from $f(x+\epsilon) = f(x) + \epsilon f'(x) + \frac{\epsilon^2}{2} f''(x) + \frac{\epsilon^3}{6} f'''(x)$ cancels all even-power terms:

$$f(x+\epsilon) - f(x-\epsilon) = 2\epsilon f'(x) + \frac{\epsilon^3}{3} f'''(x) + \mathcal{O}(\epsilon^5)$$

Dividing by $2\epsilon$ gives $f'(x) + \frac{\epsilon^2}{6} f'''(x) = f'(x) + \mathcal{O}(\epsilon^2)$. For forward differences, the $\frac{\epsilon}{2} f''(x)$ term remains uncancelled.

Problem 3: GradCheck Failure at Non-Differentiable Kinks (Hard)

Explain why evaluating GradCheck on $\text{ReLU}(x)$ at $x = 0$ yields a 100% relative error despite bug-free code.

Solution:

At $x=0$, analytical subgradient in software is $0.0$, but central difference calculates $\frac{\max(0, \epsilon) - \max(0, -\epsilon)}{2\epsilon} = \frac{\epsilon - 0}{2\epsilon} = 0.5$. The relative error is $\frac{|0.5 - 0.0|}{0.5} = 1.0$ (100%).

Therefore, numerical gradient checks must always be conducted at test points situated away from non-smooth kinks.
