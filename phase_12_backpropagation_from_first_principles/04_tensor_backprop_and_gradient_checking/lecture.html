Topic 12.4: Matrix/Tensor Backpropagation & Gradient Checking

The Vectorization Imperative: Production deep learning operates on batches of multidimensional tensors executed via GEMM kernels on GPUs. Mastering matrix calculus formulas for affine layers and validating them with two-sided finite difference Gradient Checking is mandatory for custom ML engineering.

1. The 12-Year-Old Analogy: Laser Beam vs. Wooden Ruler

The Laser Beam (Analytical Tensor Backprop): A supercomputer laser measures rocket stress across 1,000 coordinates in a microsecond. Blazing fast, but a single software sign error could quietly invert the result.

The Wooden Ruler (GradCheck): You physically push the part forward by a tiny hair ($\epsilon = 0.001$), then pull it backward ($-\epsilon$), and measure with a heavy wooden ruler. It takes 100 times longer, but if the physical measurement and laser agree to 7 decimal places, your rocket won't explode!

2. Matrix Calculus for Linear Layer: $Y = XW + b$

Given incoming adjoint $\bar{Y} \equiv \frac{\partial L}{\partial Y} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times D_{\text{out}}}$:

$$\boxed{\frac{\partial L}{\partial W} = X^T \bar{Y} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{in}} \times D_{\text{out}}}}$$ $$\boxed{\frac{\partial L}{\partial X} = \bar{Y} W^T \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times D_{\text{in}}}}$$ $$\boxed{\frac{\partial L}{\partial b} = \sum_{i=1}^B \bar{Y}_{i, :} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times D_{\text{out}}}}$$

3. Two-Sided Central Differences

Taylor expansion shows that one-sided differences have first-order error $\mathcal{O}(\epsilon)$, whereas central differences eliminate the second-order term, yielding quadratic truncation error $\mathcal{O}(\epsilon^2)$:

$$g_{\text{central}} = \frac{f(\theta + \epsilon) - f(\theta - \epsilon)}{2\epsilon} = f'(\theta) + \mathcal{O}(\epsilon^2)$$

4. Relative Error Metric & Tolerance Guide

$$\text{RelErr} = \frac{\|g_{\text{analytical}} - g_{\text{numerical}}\|_F}{\|g_{\text{analytical}}\|_F + \|g_{\text{numerical}}\|_F + \epsilon_{\text{denom}}}$$
Relative Error Status Interpretation
$\text{RelErr} < 10^{-7}$ PASS (Flawless) Exact mathematical and numerical agreement.
$10^{-7} \le \text{RelErr} < 10^{-5}$ ACCEPTABLE Minor floating-point rounding or near activation kink.
$\text{RelErr} \ge 10^{-4}$ FAIL Probable index swap, missing transpose, or sign error.
