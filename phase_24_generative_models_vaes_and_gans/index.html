Phase 24

Generative Models: VAEs & GANs

From Tractable Latent Manifolds to Adversarial Game Theory & Optimal Transport

Architectural Paradigm

While discriminative models compute conditional probabilities $P(y \mid x)$, generative models approximate the true high-dimensional data distribution $P(x)$. Phase 24 covers the mathematical foundations of generative deep learning: the taxonomy of explicit versus implicit models, autoregressive factorizations, the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) and analytical Gaussian KL derivations, pathwise derivatives via the Reparameterization Trick, minimax game theory and optimal discriminator derivations, and the resolution of manifold discontinuity via Wasserstein-1 Distance and Gradient Penalties (WGAN-GP).

02. Variational Autoencoders & The Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO)

The intractable true posterior $p(z \mid x) = p(x \mid z)p(z) / p(x)$. Variational inference with recognition model $q_\phi(z \mid x)$. 03. The Reparameterization Trick & Latent Space Geometry

The stochastic node backpropagation barrier: why $\nabla_\phi \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[f(z)]$ cannot backpropagate through sampling. 04. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) & Minimax Dynamics

Two-player zero-sum game: $\min_G \max_D V(D, G)$. Analytical derivation of the optimal discriminator $D^*(x) = \frac{p_{\text{data}}(x)}{p_{\text{data}}(x) + p_g(x)}$. Proof that at $D^*$ the objective reduces to $2 \cdot \text{JSD}(p_{\text{data}} \parallel p_g) - \log 4$. Vanishing gradients of saturated $\log(1-D)$ vs. non-saturating $-\log D$ heuristic. Mode collapse mechanics.

05. Wasserstein GAN & Gradient Penalty (WGAN-GP)

The Manifold Hypothesis and the failure of KL and JS divergences on non-overlapping sub-manifolds (constant JSD, zero gradient).
