Phase 26: Reinforcement Learning

From formal Markov Decision Processes and Bellman contractions to Deep Q-Networks, Policy Gradients, and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO).

01. Markov Decision Processes & Formal Framework

The formal MDP 5-tuple $\langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{P}, \mathcal{R}, \gamma \rangle$, the Markov Property, trajectory probability distributions, discounted returns $G_t$, state-value function $V^\pi(s)$, action-value function $Q^\pi(s, a)$, and exact analytical matrix inversion $(\mathbf{I} - \gamma \mathbf{P}^\pi)^{-1}\mathbf{r}^\pi$.

02. Bellman Equations & Dynamic Programming

Bellman Expectation and Optimality Equations for $V$ and $Q$. The Banach Fixed-Point Theorem and full mathematical proof that the Bellman operator is a $\gamma$-contraction mapping under the $\ell_\infty$ norm ($\|\mathcal{T}^* U - \mathcal{T}^* V\|_\infty \le \gamma \|U - V\|_\infty$). Policy Evaluation, Policy Iteration, and Value Iteration with convergence guarantees.

03. Temporal Difference Learning, Q-Learning & DQN

Model-free learning: Monte Carlo vs Temporal Difference (TD(0)) error $\delta_t = R_{t+1} + \gamma V(S_{t+1}) - V(S_t)$. On-policy SARSA vs off-policy Q-Learning. The Deadly Triad, and Deep Q-Networks (DQN, Mnih et al. 2015) using Experience Replay buffers $\mathcal{D}$ and periodic Target Networks $Q_{\theta^-}$.

04. The Policy Gradient Theorem & REINFORCE

Value-based vs Policy-based paradigms. Full mathematical derivation of the Policy Gradient Theorem using the log-derivative trick $\nabla_\theta \pi_\theta = \pi_\theta \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta$, trajectory distribution decomposition, causality reward-to-go, the REINFORCE algorithm, and proof that state-dependent baselines $b(s)$ yield zero bias with massive variance reduction.

05. Actor-Critic Architectures & Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)

Actor-Critic topology (Actor $\pi_\theta$, Critic $V_\phi$), 1-step TD advantages, Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE($\gamma, \lambda$)) trading off bias vs variance, Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO), and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO, Schulman et al. 2017) with clipped surrogate objective $\mathcal{L}^{\text{CLIP}}$.
