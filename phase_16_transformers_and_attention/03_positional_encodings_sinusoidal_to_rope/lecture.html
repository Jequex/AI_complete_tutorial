Topic 16.3: Positional Encodings: Sinusoidal to RoPE

From permutation-invariant sets to relative rotational geometry in the complex plane.

1. Executive Summary & The 12-Year-Old Analogy

Self-attention is mathematically permutation equivariant. If you shuffle the input words, the attention outputs are identical (just shuffled in the same order). A vanilla Transformer cannot tell "dog bites man" from "man bites dog"!

To teach the model word order, we assign each token a mathematical timestamp. This lecture explores the transition from Vaswani Sinusoidal Positional Encodings (2017) to Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) (Su et al. 2021) powering modern open-weight LLMs like LLaMA 3, Mistral, and Gemma.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: Clocks Ticking at Different Speeds & Spinning Dancers

  • The Grandfather Clock Gearbox (Sinusoidal Encodings): Imagine giving each student in line a watch with 128 hands. The 1st hand spins in 2 seconds; the 2nd hand in 10 seconds; the last hand takes 10,000 hours. The exact collection of angles is a unique signature for position 45 that never repeats.
  • The Spinning Dancers (Rotary Position Embedding - RoPE): Instead of adding heavy clock numbers to each dancer, each dancer spins by angle $m \times \theta$. When dancer $m$ and dancer $n$ reach out, the angle between their hands depends strictly on the relative distance $(m - n)$!

2. Vaswani Sinusoidal Positional Encodings (2017)

$$PE_{(pos, 2i)} = \sin\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}\right), \quad PE_{(pos, 2i+1)} = \cos\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}\right)$$

The Relative Position Shift Property & Proof

Using the trigonometric angle addition identities:

$$\sin(\alpha + \beta) = \sin\alpha \cos\beta + \cos\alpha \sin\beta$$ $$\cos(\alpha + \beta) = \cos\alpha \cos\beta - \sin\alpha \sin\beta$$

Letting $\alpha = pos \cdot \omega_i$ and $\beta = k \cdot \omega_i$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} PE_{(pos + k, 2i)} \\ PE_{(pos + k, 2i+1)} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(k\omega_i) & \sin(k\omega_i) \\ -\sin(k\omega_i) & \cos(k\omega_i) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} PE_{(pos, 2i)} \\ PE_{(pos, 2i+1)} \end{bmatrix}$$

The matrix $M(k)$ is a 2D rotation matrix depending strictly on offset $k$ and frequency $\omega_i$, with zero dependence on the absolute coordinate $pos$.

3. Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) (Su et al. 2021)

In modern LLMs (LLaMA 3, Gemma, Mistral), instead of adding positional noise to embeddings at the input layer, RoPE multiplies Queries and Keys by orthogonal 2D rotation matrices:

$$\tilde{\mathbf{q}}_m = R_{\theta, m} \mathbf{q}, \quad \tilde{\mathbf{k}}_n = R_{\theta, n} \mathbf{k}$$

In the complex plane ($q e^{i m \theta}$ and $k e^{i n \theta}$):

$$\langle \tilde{\mathbf{q}}_m, \tilde{\mathbf{k}}_n \rangle = \operatorname{Re}\left( (q e^{i m \theta})(k e^{i n \theta})^* \right) = \operatorname{Re}\left( (q k^*) e^{i (m - n)\theta} \right)$$
$$\langle R_{\theta, m} \mathbf{q}, R_{\theta, n} \mathbf{k} \rangle = g(\mathbf{q}, \mathbf{k}, m - n)$$

The attention score between query $m$ and key $n$ depends purely on the relative distance $(m - n)$, preserving vector norm ($\|R x\| = \|x\|$) and generalizing smoothly to long contexts.
