Problem 1: Manual Calculation of Sinusoidal Encodings (Easy)

For $d = 4$, $\omega_0 = 1.0, \omega_1 = 0.01$:

  1. Compute $PE_0, PE_1, PE_2$.
  2. Compute dot products $\langle PE_0, PE_1 \rangle$ and $\langle PE_0, PE_2 \rangle$.
  3. Verify distance decay.
View Complete Solution

Step 1:

$$PE_0 = [0.0, 1.0, 0.0, 1.0]$$ $$PE_1 = [0.8415, 0.5403, 0.0100, 1.0000]$$ $$PE_2 = [0.9093, -0.4161, 0.0200, 0.9998]$$

Step 2: $\langle PE_0, PE_1 \rangle = 1.5403, \quad \langle PE_0, PE_2 \rangle = 0.5837$.

Step 3: As distance increases from 0 to 1 to 2, inner product decays monotonically from $2.0 \to 1.5403 \to 0.5837$.

Problem 2: 2D RoPE Rotation and Relative Dot Product Verification (Medium)

Let $\theta = \frac{\pi}{6}$ rad ($30^\circ$), $q = [1, 0]^T$, $k = [1, 0]^T$. Rotate $q$ at $m = 3$ and $k$ at $n = 1$, and compute their inner product.

View Complete Solution

Angle for $q$: $3 \times \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2} \implies \tilde{q}_3 = [0, 1]^T$.

Angle for $k$: $1 \times \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{6} \implies \tilde{k}_1 = [\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}]^T$.

Dot product: $\langle \tilde{q}_3, \tilde{k}_1 \rangle = 0 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 0.5$.

By RoPE formula: $\cos((3 - 1)\theta) = \cos(\pi/3) = 0.5$. Matched!

Problem 3: Group-Theoretic Structure of Positional Shifts (Hard)

Prove that $M_i(k)^T M_i(k) = I_2$ and $M_i(k_1)M_i(k_2) = M_i(k_1 + k_2)$.

View Complete Solution

Using angle addition identities, the matrix product $M_i(k_1)M_i(k_2)$ yields $\cos((k_1 + k_2)\omega_i)$ and $\sin((k_1 + k_2)\omega_i)$ across all 4 entries. This forms an abelian group isomorphic to $(\mathbb{Z}, +)$, allowing neural attention to compose relative displacements linearly.
