The Four Pillars of the Transformer Revolution

1. Scaled Attention

$\mathcal{O}(1)$ path length soft information retrieval scaled by $1/\sqrt{d_k}$ to prevent gradient collapse.

2. Multi-Head Projections

Simultaneously attending to syntactic, semantic, and coreference subspaces without FLOP penalty.

3. Rotational Coordinates

RoPE complex plane rotations encoding relative distance directly into attention inner products.

4. Causal Pre-LN Decoders

Upper-triangular masking for autoregressive generation coupled with non-vanishing residual gradient highways.
