Problem 1: Manual Calculation of Causal Masked Attention (Easy)

For logits $S = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & 5 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$:

  1. Construct the upper-triangular mask matrix $M$.
  2. Compute the masked logits $Z = S + M$.
  3. Compute row-wise softmax probabilities and confirm future tokens receive exactly 0 probability.
View Complete Solution

Step 1: $M_{ij} = -\infty$ for $j > i$ and $0$ otherwise.

Step 2: $Z = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -\infty & -\infty \\ 3 & 1 & -\infty \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$.

Step 3:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0000 & 0.0000 & 0.0000 \\ 0.8808 & 0.1192 & 0.0000 \\ 0.0634 & 0.4683 & 0.4683 \end{bmatrix}$$

Every future token receives exact zero weight ($0.0$), ensuring zero information leakage during autoregressive decoding.

Problem 2: Attention Memory Scaling & FlashAttention Motivation (Medium)

Calculate the memory in gigabytes required to hold the attention matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times h \times T \times T}$ in FP16 for $B = 4, h = 32$ at $T = 2,048$ vs $T = 65,536$.

View Complete Solution

Formula: $\text{Bytes} = B \times h \times T^2 \times 2$.

For $T = 2,048$: $4 \times 32 \times 2048^2 \times 2 = 1.07\text{ GB}$ per layer.

For $T = 65,536$: sequence length is $32\times$ longer, so memory explodes by $32^2 = 1024\times \to 1.0\text{ TB}$ per layer, or $32.8\text{ TB}$ for 32 layers!

FlashAttention tiles softmax inside high-speed SRAM, eliminating this quadratic HBM bottleneck.

Problem 3: Residual Stream Gradient Preservation: Pre-LN vs Post-LN (Hard)

Prove that Pre-LN creates an identity path $I$ in the loss gradient $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial x^{(0)}}$, shielding deep networks from vanishing gradients.

View Complete Solution

Pre-LN recurrence expands to: $x^{(L)} = x^{(0)} + \sum_{l=0}^{L-1} \mathcal{F}_l(\text{LN}(x^{(l)}))$.

Differentiating with respect to $x^{(0)}$ yields:

$$\frac{\partial x^{(L)}}{\partial x^{(0)}} = I + \sum_{l=0}^{L-1} \frac{\partial \mathcal{F}_l}{\partial x^{(0)}}$$

The identity matrix $I$ acts as an unobstructed highway that channels gradients directly back to the initial input layer, preventing the gradient attenuation inherent to Post-LN.
