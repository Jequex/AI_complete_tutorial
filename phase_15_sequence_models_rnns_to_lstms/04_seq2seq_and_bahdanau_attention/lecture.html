Topic 15.4: Seq2Seq Architecture & Bahdanau Additive Attention

Overcoming the fixed information bottleneck with content-based dynamic alignment and attention matrices.

Executive Summary

The Sequence-to-Sequence (Seq2Seq) framework maps variable-length inputs to variable-length outputs using an Encoder and an Autoregressive Decoder. However, compressing the entire source sequence into a single static context vector $c = h_{T_x}$ creates a severe Information Bottleneck that degrades performance on long sequences. In 2014, Bahdanau et al. introduced Additive Attention: giving the decoder a soft, content-based alignment mechanism that selectively queries intermediate encoder annotations, generating a customized context vector $c_i = \sum_j \alpha_{ij} h_j$ for each decoding step.

1. The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Simultaneous Translator with a Magic Spotlight

  • Vanilla Seq2Seq (The Memory Test): A diplomat delivers a 50-word speech. You must listen to the whole speech with your eyes shut, condense the entire speech into one single number in your head, and then recite it in English from memory. You easily forget details from the beginning of the sentence!
  • Bahdanau Attention (The Magic Spotlight): The diplomat's speech is printed out in front of you. When you are about to speak an English word, you shine a magic spotlight ($e_{ij}$) over the paper. The French words that correspond to your next word light up with high intensity ($\alpha_{ij}$), allowing you to glance directly at them as you translate!

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Domain Description
$h_j \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$ Vector Space Encoder annotation vector representing source position $j \in \{1, \dots, T_x\}$
$s_{i-1} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_s}$ Vector Space Previous decoder hidden state (query vector) at target step $i$
$e_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}$ Scalar Energy Alignment energy score: $v_a^T \tanh(W_a s_{i-1} + U_a h_j + b_a)$
$\alpha_{ij} \in [0, 1]$ Softmax Probability Attention weight indicating relevance of source position $j$ to target step $i$
$c_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$ Vector Space Dynamic context vector: $\sum_{j=1}^{T_x} \alpha_{ij} h_j$
$s_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_s}$ Vector Space Updated decoder state: $f(s_{i-1}, y_{i-1}, c_i)$

3. The Bahdanau Attention Equations

1. Alignment Energy:

$$e_{ij} = v_a^T \tanh(W_a s_{i-1} + U_a h_j + b_a)$$

2. Attention Weight Distribution:

$$\boxed{\alpha_{ij} = \frac{\exp(e_{ij})}{\sum_{k=1}^{T_x} \exp(e_{ik})}}$$

3. Dynamic Context Vector:

$$\boxed{c_i = \sum_{j=1}^{T_x} \alpha_{ij} h_j}$$

4. The Information Bottleneck: Mathematical Analysis

In vanilla Seq2Seq, the bottleneck channel capacity is fixed: $\mathcal{C} = d \times 32$ bits. While input semantic entropy grows as $\mathcal{O}(T_x)$, the capacity remains $\mathcal{O}(1)$. Bahdanau Attention expands available memory to $\mathcal{O}(T_x)$ by storing all hidden states $\{h_j\}_{j=1}^{T_x}$ and accessing them via differentiable content-based queries.
