Topic 15.3: Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) & Bidirectionality

Streamlined recurrent gating, 25% parameter reduction, and dual-direction temporal feature fusion.

Executive Summary

The Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) (Cho et al., 2014) simplifies the LSTM by eliminating the separate cell state ($c_t$) and fusing the forget and input gates into a single Update Gate ($z_t$). This yields a $25\%$ reduction in trainable weights with comparable capacity. Meanwhile, Bidirectional RNNs (BiRNN) break the causal constraint by processing sequences in both forward and backward directions, capturing both past history and future context.

1. The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Sports Car & Reading Backwards

  • The GRU (The Aerodynamic Sports Car): The LSTM is like a heavy armored truck with an iron safe ($c_t$) and 4 guards. The GRU removes the heavy safe and manages everything with a single slider lever ($z_t$): slide left to keep yesterday's notes, slide right to overwrite with today's observations. It's faster, lighter, and saves 25% fuel.
  • Bidirectionality (Reading a Murder Mystery): If you read a mystery novel forward, you don't know who the butler is until the end. If you have a friend reading from the back of the book backwards simultaneously, by chapter 5 you know both the clues from the past and the reveal in the future. That is a Bidirectional RNN.

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

Symbol Domain Description
$r_t \in (0, 1)^{d_h}$ Sigmoid Gate Reset Gate: controls how much past hidden state $h_{t-1}$ affects candidate $\tilde{h}_t$
$z_t \in (0, 1)^{d_h}$ Sigmoid Gate Update Gate: linear interpolation factor between $h_{t-1}$ and $\tilde{h}_t$
$\tilde{h}_t \in (-1, 1)^{d_h}$ $\tanh$ State Candidate Hidden State: newly generated prospective features
$\vec{h}_t, \overleftarrow{h}_t$ Vector Space Forward pass state ($1 \to t$) and backward pass state ($T \to t$)
$h_t = [\vec{h}_t; \overleftarrow{h}_t]$ Concatenation Merged bidirectional representation $\in \mathbb{R}^{2 d_h}$

3. The Gated Recurrent Unit Formulation

The standard GRU equations are:

$$r_t = \sigma(W_r [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_r)$$ $$z_t = \sigma(W_z [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_z)$$ $$\tilde{h}_t = \tanh(W_{hh} (r_t \odot h_{t-1}) + W_{xh} x_t + b_h)$$ $$\boxed{h_t = (1 - z_t) \odot h_{t-1} + z_t \odot \tilde{h}_t}$$

5. Bidirectional Sequence Integration

Breaking Causal Blindness

A causal RNN only knows $x_{\le t}$. For sequence labeling, reading right-to-left ($\overleftarrow{h}_t$) simultaneously provides immediate knowledge of upcoming tokens, enabling disambiguation:

$$h_t^{\text{Bi}} = [\vec{h}_t; \overleftarrow{h}_t] \in \mathbb{R}^{2 d_h}$$
