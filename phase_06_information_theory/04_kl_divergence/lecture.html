Topic 6.4: Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence

Measuring Statistical Asymmetry, Forward vs. Reverse KL, VAE Loss Derivations, and RLHF Alignment.

🧒 1. Intuitive Analogy: The Outdated Mountain Map

Imagine you are hiking through a mountain range.

  • Terrain Reality ($P$): Heavy rain washed away an old trail and created a new river.
  • Your Map ($Q$): You bought a cheap paper map printed in 1960.

Every time your map says "straight path" but you hit a roaring river, you have to stop, backtrack, and take a 2-hour detour. The extra sweat and wasted time you spend because your map ($Q$) did not match reality ($P$) is the Kullback-Leibler Divergence $D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q)$.

Why it is NOT a "Distance": Walking in a real city with a video game map is totally different from playing a video game using a real city map! KL Divergence is asymmetric: $D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q) \ne D_{\text{KL}}(Q \parallel P)$.

📐 3. Definition & Fundamental Triad

Definition of Discrete KL Divergence

$$D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q) = \sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} P(x) \log_2 \left( \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \right)$$

The Fundamental Triad

$$H(P, Q) = H(P) + D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q)$$

Cross-Entropy equals the intrinsic entropy of reality plus the penalty for having an imperfect model. Minimizing Cross-Entropy is mathematically identical to minimizing KL Divergence!

🛡️ 4. Gibbs' Inequality Proof

Theorem: Non-Negativity of Relative Entropy

$$D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q) \ge 0$$

Using $\ln(u) \le u - 1$ with $u = \frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}$:

$$-D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q) = \sum_{x} P(x) \ln\left(\frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}\right) \le \sum_{x} P(x) \left( \frac{Q(x)}{P(x)} - 1 \right) = \sum_{x} Q(x) - \sum_{x} P(x) = 1 - 1 = 0$$

Multiplying by $-1$ gives $D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q) \ge 0$. $\blacksquare$

⚖️ 5. Forward KL vs. Reverse KL

FeatureForward KL: $D_{\text{KL}}(P \parallel Q)$Reverse KL: $D_{\text{KL}}(Q \parallel P)$
Expectation$\mathbb{E}_{x \sim P}[\ln(P / Q)]$$\mathbb{E}_{x \sim Q}[\ln(Q / P)]$
BehaviorMode-Covering (Zero-Avoiding): Stretches out to cover all modes of $P$.Mode-Seeking (Zero-Forcing): Locks onto a single mode and ignores others.
AI ApplicationsSupervised Pretraining, Maximum Likelihood.Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), RLHF, DPO.

🎨 6. Analytical Gaussian KL & VAE Latent Loss

Gaussian KL Divergence

$$D_{\text{KL}}(\mathcal{N}(\mu_0, \sigma_0^2) \parallel \mathcal{N}(\mu_1, \sigma_1^2)) = \ln\left(\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_0}\right) + \frac{\sigma_0^2 + (\mu_0 - \mu_1)^2}{2\sigma_1^2} - \frac{1}{2}$$

The Canonical VAE Loss Term:

Matching latent distribution $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ to standard normal prior $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{VAE\_KL}} = -\frac{1}{2} \sum_{j=1}^d \left( 1 + \ln(\sigma_j^2) - \mu_j^2 - \sigma_j^2 \right)$$
