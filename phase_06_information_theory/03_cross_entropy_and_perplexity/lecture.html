Topic 6.3: Cross-Entropy & Perplexity

The Universal Loss Engine of Deep Learning & Language Model Evaluation.

🧒 1. Intuitive Analogy: The 20 Questions Game with the Wrong Rulebook

Imagine playing 20 Questions with a visitor from Mars:

  • Earth Reality ($P$): Dogs and cats are everywhere. Dragons do not exist.
  • Martian Beliefs ($Q$): The Martian assumes dragons are very common and dogs are rare!

When trying to guess the mystery pet, the Martian asks wasteful questions first: "Does it breathe fire?". They waste valuable guesses because their model $Q$ does not match reality $P$.

  • Entropy $H(P)$: The minimum questions needed if you have a perfect model of reality.
  • Cross-Entropy $H(P, Q)$: The number of questions you actually ask when reality follows $P$, but your strategy uses model $Q$.

Training an AI: Training is simply twisting the AI's internal model $Q$ until it matches human reality $P$, minimizing cross-entropy down to the theoretical limit $H(P)$!

🔣 2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder Table

📐 3. Mathematical Formulation

Formal Definition of Cross-Entropy

$$H(P, Q) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} P(x) \log_2 Q(x) \quad \text{(bits)}$$ $$H_e(P, Q) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} P(x) \ln Q(x) \quad \text{(nats)}$$

⚡ 4. The One-Hot Case in Deep Learning

In supervised classification and next-token prediction, ground-truth label $y^*$ has probability $1.0$, and all other words have probability $0$. The cross-entropy instantly simplifies to:

Negative Log-Likelihood Loss (NLL)

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}} = -\ln Q(y^*)$$

If the AI puts $100\%$ probability on the correct token ($Q=1.0$), loss is $-\ln(1.0) = 0$. If the AI assigns only $1\%$ probability ($Q=0.01$), loss is $-\ln(0.01) \approx 4.61$. The loss punishes bad predictions logarithmically!

🎲 5. Perplexity (PPL): The Benchmark of Modern LLMs

Definition of Perplexity

$$\text{PPL} = \exp\left( -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln Q(y_i) \right) = e^{\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}}$$

The "Die Roll" Intuition:

A model with a perplexity of $\mathbf{10}$ predicts the next word with the same average uncertainty as if it were choosing uniformly from a bag of 10 equally likely words.

  • Untrained Model (32k vocab): $\text{PPL} = 32,000$ (complete confusion).
  • Modern State-of-the-Art LLM (Llama, GPT-4): $\text{PPL} \approx 6 - 15$ on standard benchmarks.

🌡️ 6. Temperature Scaling in Softmax

Logits to Probabilities

$$Q(x_i) = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_{j} \exp(z_j / T)}$$
  • $T \to 0$ (Low Temperature): Greedy mode. The highest logit wins 100% of the mass. High confidence, zero randomness.
  • $T = 1.0$ (Default): Standard calibrated model confidence.
  • $T > 1.0$ (High Temperature): Flattens the distribution towards uniform $\frac{1}{K}$. Maximum diversity, but higher risk of hallucinations.
