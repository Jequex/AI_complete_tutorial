NLP & Language Model Metrics

Perplexity, BLEU-4 with Brevity Penalties, ROUGE via Dynamic Programming LCS, Exact Match, and Token F1.

1. The 12-Year-Old Intuition: Grading a Student's Translation

Suppose a human teacher provides the reference translation: "The black cat sleeps peacefully on the soft carpet."

A bilingual student writes: "The dark cat is resting quietly on the soft rug."

How do we grade this automatically?

  • Perplexity ($PPL$): Asks how surprised the model was to see these words. A perplexity of 10 means the AI had to guess between 10 equally probable words at every step. Lower is better.
  • BLEU: Checks if the words produced by the student were in the teacher's sentence (Precision). Punishes repetitive spam like "the the the" and overly short answers.
  • ROUGE: Checks how much of the teacher's key ideas were captured (Recall). Perfect for summarization.
  • Exact Match & Token F1: Normalizes text (stripping punctuation and articles) to grade factual question answering.

3. The BLEU Metric (Papineni et al., 2002)

Modified clipped precision prevents repetitive phrase hacking by capping $n$-gram counts at their maximum frequency in any reference:

$$p_n = \frac{\sum_{C} \sum_{\text{gram} \in C} \text{Count}_{\text{clip}}(\text{gram})}{\sum_{C} \sum_{\text{gram} \in C} \text{Count}_{\text{cand}}(\text{gram})}$$

The Brevity Penalty ($BP$) penalizes candidates that are shorter than reference length $r$:

$$BP = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } c > r \\ \exp\left(1 - \frac{r}{c}\right) & \text{if } c \le r \end{cases}$$ $$\boxed{\text{BLEU}-4 = BP \cdot \exp\left(\sum_{n=1}^4 \frac{1}{4} \log p_n\right)}$$

4. ROUGE & Dynamic Programming LCS

ROUGE-L uses the Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) computed via 2D Dynamic Programming in $\mathcal{O}(N \cdot M)$ time:

$$L[i, j] = \begin{cases} 0 & i=0 \text{ or } j=0 \\ L[i-1, j-1] + 1 & c_i = r_j \\ \max(L[i-1, j], L[i, j-1]) & c_i \ne r_j \end{cases}$$ $$R_{\text{LCS}} = \frac{\text{LCS}(C, R)}{|R|}, \qquad P_{\text{LCS}} = \frac{\text{LCS}(C, R)}{|C|}, \qquad \boxed{F_{\text{LCS}} = \frac{2 R_{\text{LCS}} P_{\text{LCS}}}{R_{\text{LCS}} + P_{\text{LCS}}}}$$

5. SQuAD Normalization & Token F1

Before exact matching, text is normalized by: (1) lowercasing, (2) stripping punctuation, (3) stripping articles (a, an, the), and (4) whitespace collapsing. Token F1 computes harmonic mean over matching token multisets:

$$\boxed{F_1^{\text{token}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{Precision}_{\text{token}} \cdot \text{Recall}_{\text{token}}}{\text{Precision}_{\text{token}} + \text{Recall}_{\text{token}}}}$$
