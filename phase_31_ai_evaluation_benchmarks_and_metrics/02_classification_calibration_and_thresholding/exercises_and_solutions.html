Exercise 1: Exact ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and the Mann-Whitney $U$ Equivalence

A benchmark of 10 samples has ground-truth labels and model scores: $y = [1, 1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0]$ with sorted scores $\hat{p} = [0.95, 0.90, 0.85, 0.70, 0.60, 0.55, 0.45, 0.35, 0.25, 0.10]$.

Tasks:

  1. Compute $\text{AUC}_{\text{ROC}}$ via trapezoidal integration.
  2. Count positive-negative pairwise comparisons to compute Mann-Whitney $U / (N_+ N_-)$ and verify equivalence.
  3. Compute Precision-Recall curve operating points and calculate Average Precision ($\text{PR-AUC}$).
View Complete Solution

1. Trapezoidal ROC-AUC: Summing trapezoid slices across unique thresholds gives $\text{AUC}_{\text{ROC}} = 0.08 + 0.16 + 0.16 + 0.20 + 0.20 = \mathbf{0.80}$.

2. Mann-Whitney $U$ Count:

  • Positive 0.95 beats 5 negatives → 5
  • Positive 0.90 beats 5 negatives → 5
  • Positive 0.70 beats 4 negatives → 4
  • Positive 0.60 beats 4 negatives → 4
  • Positive 0.35 beats 2 negatives → 2
$$U = 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 2 = 20 \implies \frac{U}{N_+ N_-} = \frac{20}{5 \times 5} = \mathbf{0.80} \quad (\text{Exact Match})$$

3. PR-AUC: $\text{PR-AUC} = \sum \Delta R_k \cdot P_k = 0.2(1.0 + 1.0 + 0.75 + 0.80 + 0.625) = \mathbf{0.835}$.

Exercise 2: Murphy's Brier Score Decomposition

Given 4 bins covering $N=20$ instances with overall base rate $\bar{y} = 0.45$:

BinCount $n_m$$\bar{p}_m$$\bar{y}_m$
160.100.1667
240.350.2500
350.650.6000
450.890.8000

Tasks: Compute Reliability ($\text{REL}$), Resolution ($\text{RES}$), Uncertainty ($\text{UNC}$), and total Brier Score.

View Complete Solution

1. Reliability: $\text{REL} = \sum \frac{n_m}{N}(\bar{p}_m - \bar{y}_m)^2 \approx \mathbf{0.005985}$.

2. Resolution: $\text{RES} = \sum \frac{n_m}{N}(\bar{y}_m - \bar{y})^2 \approx \mathbf{0.068334}$.

3. Uncertainty: $\text{UNC} = \bar{y}(1 - \bar{y}) = 0.45 \times 0.55 = \mathbf{0.247500}$.

4. Total Brier Score: $\text{BS} = \text{REL} - \text{RES} + \text{UNC} = 0.005985 - 0.068334 + 0.247500 = \mathbf{0.185151}$.

Exercise 3: ECE and Temperature Scaling Invariance

An overconfident model outputs raw probabilities $\hat{p} = [0.90, 0.85, 0.80, 0.20, 0.15, 0.10]$ for labels $y = [1, 1, 0, 1, 0, 0]$ with raw logits $z = [+2.20, +1.73, +1.39, -1.39, -1.73, -2.20]$.

Tasks:

  1. Compute raw ECE across 2 equal bins ($[0.0, 0.5]$ and $(0.5, 1.0]$).
  2. Apply temperature scaling $T = 2.0$ to compute new probabilities and the calibrated ECE.
  3. Prove why ROC-AUC and 0.5-threshold accuracy do not change.
View Complete Solution

1. Raw ECE: Bin 1 gap = $|0.3333 - 0.150| = 0.1833$; Bin 2 gap = $|0.6667 - 0.850| = 0.1833$ → $\text{ECE}_{\text{raw}} = \mathbf{18.33\%}$.

2. Calibrated with $T = 2.0$:

Calibrated probabilities become $\hat{p}' \approx [0.7503, 0.7037, 0.6671, 0.3329, 0.2963, 0.2497]$.

Bin 1 gap = $|0.3333 - 0.2930| = 0.0403$; Bin 2 gap = $|0.6667 - 0.7070| = 0.0403$ → $\text{ECE}_{\text{cal}} = \mathbf{4.03\%}$ ($4.5\times$ improvement!).

3. Invariance: Because dividing by $T > 0$ preserves the sign ($z/T > 0 \iff z > 0$) and the strict ranking of logits ($z_a > z_b \iff z_a/T > z_b/T$), the 0.5-threshold binary predictions and the rank order of pairs are mathematically identical.
