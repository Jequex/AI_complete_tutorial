Exercise 1: Atomic Fact Decomposition & FActScore

A generated passage states that computer scientist Geoffrey Hinton was born in London in 1947, earned his Edinburgh PhD in 1978, co-founded DeepMind with Demis Hassabis in 2012, won the Turing Award in 2018 with Bengio and LeCun, and left Google in 2023.

Tasks: Decompose into $K=12$ atomic propositions, verify against the verified knowledge base (Hinton co-founded DNNresearch, not DeepMind), and compute Claim-Level Precision, Hallucination Rate, and Document-Level Hallucination status.

View Complete Solution

1. Entailment Verification:

10 claims are supported by ground truth. 2 claims are hallucinated (claims 6 and 7 falsely linking Hinton as a co-founder of DeepMind).

2. Metrics:

$$\text{FActScore (Precision)} = \frac{10}{12} \approx \mathbf{83.33\%}$$ $$H_{\text{claim}} = 1 - 0.8333 = \frac{2}{12} \approx \mathbf{16.67\%}$$ $$H_{\text{doc}} = \mathbf{1.0 \text{ (100\% contaminated)}}$$

Exercise 2: Prompt Perturbation & Robustness Degradation

A medical QA benchmark achieves clean accuracy $\mathcal{S}_{\text{clean}} = 88.0\%$. Under character typos, accuracy drops to $74.8\%$; under adversarial distractors, accuracy drops to $57.2\%$.

Tasks: Compute robustness retention coefficients $\rho_{\text{typo}}$ and $\rho_{\text{distr}}$, and identify the primary architectural failure mode.

View Complete Solution
$$\rho_{\text{typo}} = \frac{74.8\%}{88.0\%} \approx \mathbf{0.8500 \text{ (85.0\%)}}$$ $$\rho_{\text{distr}} = \frac{57.2\%}{88.0\%} \approx \mathbf{0.6500 \text{ (65.0\%)}}$$

Failure Mode: Adversarial distractors cause a catastrophic $35\%$ drop ($\rho = 0.65$). The model exhibits attentional vulnerability to irrelevant preamble statements, highlighting the need for hard-negative context fine-tuning.

Exercise 3: Goodhart's Law Taxonomy & Contamination Scanning

Tasks:

  1. Classify: (a) Empty docstring inflation for code line count; (b) Validation checkpoint outlier regression; (c) 1,500-word run-on answers under RLHF length reward; (d) Injected prompt evaluation override instructions.
  2. In a 100-question test set, 15 questions contain exact 10-gram matches in the pretraining corpus. Compute the contamination ratio.
View Complete Solution

1. Goodhart Classifications:

  • (a) Causal Goodharting: Intervening on proxy (file line count) without improving cause (logic).
  • (b) Regressive Goodharting: Selecting extreme positive validation noise; regressing in production.
  • (c) Extremal Goodharting: Moderate length-quality correlation collapsing under extreme optimization.
  • (d) Adversarial Goodharting: Prompt injection actively manipulating the judge evaluator.

2. Contamination Ratio: $\mathcal{C}_{\text{contam}} = \frac{15}{100} = \mathbf{15.0\%}$.
