Topic 34.3: Exercises & Solutions

Exercise 1: Exact Moments of Geometric Brownian Motion

For the GBM SDE $dX_t = \mu X_t dt + \sigma X_t dW_t$ with $X_0 > 0$:

  1. Apply Ito's Lemma to $f(X_t) = X_t^p$ to find $d(X_t^p)$.
  2. Derive the ODE for the $p$-th moment $m_p(t) = \mathbb{E}[X_t^p]$.
  3. Solve for $\mathbb{E}[X_t]$ and $\text{Var}(X_t)$.
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. Differential via Ito's Lemma

$$d(X_t^p) = \left(p\mu + \frac{1}{2}p(p-1)\sigma^2\right) X_t^p dt + p\sigma X_t^p dW_t$$

2. Moment ODE

$$\frac{d m_p(t)}{dt} = \left(p\mu + \frac{1}{2}p(p-1)\sigma^2\right) m_p(t)$$

3. Analytical Moments

$$\mathbb{E}[X_t] = X_0 e^{\mu t}, \quad \mathbb{E}[X_t^2] = X_0^2 e^{(2\mu + \sigma^2)t}$$ $$\text{Var}(X_t) = \mathbf{X_0^2 e^{2\mu t} (e^{\sigma^2 t} - 1)}$$

Exercise 2: VP-SDE Transition Kernel & Score Function

For the continuous diffusion VP-SDE: $d\mathbf{x} = -\frac{1}{2}\beta(t)\mathbf{x} dt + \sqrt{\beta(t)} d\mathbf{w}$.

  1. Compute conditional mean $\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_0]$ using integrating factors.
  2. Prove that total variance is conserved: $\alpha_t^2 + \sigma_t^2 = 1$.
  3. Derive the exact conditional score $\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t} \log p_{0t}(\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_0)$.
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. Conditional Mean

$$\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_0] = \mathbf{x}_0 e^{-\frac{1}{2}\int_0^t \beta(s)ds} \triangleq \alpha_t \mathbf{x}_0$$

2. Variance Conservation

$$\text{Var}(\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_0) = 1 - e^{-\int_0^t \beta(s)ds} \triangleq \sigma_t^2 \implies \alpha_t^2 + \sigma_t^2 = 1$$

3. Conditional Score

$$\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t} \log p_{0t}(\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_0) = -\frac{\mathbf{x}_t - \alpha_t \mathbf{x}_0}{\sigma_t^2} = -\frac{\mathbf{\epsilon}}{\sigma_t}$$

Exercise 3: Fokker-Planck Continuity & Detailed Balance

The Fokker-Planck equation can be written as $\frac{\partial p}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial J}{\partial x} = 0$.

  1. Express the probability current $J(x, t)$.
  2. Prove that $J(x) = 0$ in equilibrium (detailed balance).
  3. Derive the Boltzmann-Gibbs stationary density $p_\infty(x) \propto e^{-2V(x)/\sigma^2}$.
View Complete Step-by-Step Solution

1. Probability Current

$$J(x, t) = \mu(x, t) p(x, t) - \frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}[\sigma^2(x, t) p(x, t)]$$

2. Vanishing Boundary Conditions

Since $\partial J / \partial x = 0$ in equilibrium, $J(x) = C$. Because normalizable densities vanish at infinity ($p \to 0, p' \to 0$ as $x \to \pm \infty$), $C = 0$.

3. Boltzmann Equilibrium

$$\mu(x) p(x) = \frac{\sigma^2}{2} \frac{dp}{dx} \implies p_\infty(x) = \frac{1}{Z} \exp\left(-\frac{2 V(x)}{\sigma^2}\right)$$
