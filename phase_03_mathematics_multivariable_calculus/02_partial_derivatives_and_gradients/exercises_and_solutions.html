Problem 1: Gradient Derivation of Linear Regression MSE Loss

Medium

🧒 The Context: Maya and Timmy are predicting lemonade sales $\hat{y} = w x + b$ minimizing $\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{N}\sum (wx_i + b - y_i)^2$.

  1. Compute partial derivative $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial w}$.
  2. Compute partial derivative $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b}$.
  3. For a sample $(x = 2.0, y = 5.0)$ with $w = 1.0, b = 0.0$, compute $\nabla \mathcal{L}$.
Click to view solution for Problem 1
1. Partial w.r.t $w$: $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial w} = \frac{2}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (w x_i + b - y_i) x_i$$
2. Partial w.r.t $b$: $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b} = \frac{2}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (w x_i + b - y_i)$$
3. Numerical Calculation: $\hat{y} = 1.0(2.0) + 0 = 2.0$. Error $e = 2.0 - 5.0 = -3.0$. $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial w} = 2(-3.0)(2.0) = -12.0, \quad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b} = 2(-3.0) = -6.0$$ $$\nabla \mathcal{L} = \begin{bmatrix} -12.0 \\ -6.0 \end{bmatrix}$$ Negative gradient is $[+12.0, +6.0]^T$, increasing $w$ and $b$ to raise prediction toward 5.0!

Problem 2: Directional Derivatives & Orthogonal Contour Tangents

Medium

🧒 The Context: Hiking on crater lake $f(x, y) = 2x^2 + y^2$ at point $P = (1, 2)$.

  1. Compute gradient $\nabla f(1, 2)$.
  2. Compute unit direction $\mathbf{u}$ for $\mathbf{v} = [3, 4]^T$.
  3. Calculate directional derivative $D_{\mathbf{u}} f(1, 2)$.
  4. Find a flat walking direction $\mathbf{u}_{\text{flat}}$ orthogonal to $\nabla f$.
Click to view solution for Problem 2
1. Gradient: $\nabla f(x, y) = [4x, 2y]^T \implies \nabla f(1, 2) = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$.
2. Unit Direction: $\|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5 \implies \mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6 \\ 0.8 \end{bmatrix}$.
3. Directional Derivative: $$D_{\mathbf{u}} f = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 0.6 \\ 0.8 \end{bmatrix} = 2.4 + 3.2 = \mathbf{5.6}$$
4. Flat Walking Direction: $4 u_x + 4 u_y = 0 \implies u_x = -u_y$. Normalizing gives $\mathbf{u}_{\text{flat}} = \begin{bmatrix} -1/\sqrt{2} \\ 1/\sqrt{2} \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} -0.7071 \\ 0.7071 \end{bmatrix}$, which walks tangent to the contour ellipse!

Problem 3: Manual Gradient Descent Trajectory on an Elliptical Bowl

Challenging

🧒 The Context: Minimizing $f(x, y) = x^2 + 4y^2$ starting at $\mathbf{w}_0 = [4.0, 2.0]^T$ with $\eta = 0.1$.

  1. Derive coordinate update formulas.
  2. Perform two full manual iterations ($\mathbf{w}_1$ and $\mathbf{w}_2$).
  3. Verify loss strictly decreases.
Click to view solution for Problem 3
1. Update Rules: $\nabla f = [2x, 8y]^T$. $$x_{t+1} = x_t - 0.1(2x_t) = \mathbf{0.8 x_t}, \quad y_{t+1} = y_t - 0.1(8y_t) = \mathbf{0.2 y_t}$$
2. Iteration Calculations:
  • $\mathbf{w}_0 = [4.0, 2.0]^T \implies f(\mathbf{w}_0) = 4^2 + 4(2^2) = \mathbf{32.0}$
  • $\mathbf{w}_1 = [0.8(4), 0.2(2)]^T = \mathbf{[3.2, 0.4]^T} \implies f(\mathbf{w}_1) = 3.2^2 + 4(0.4^2) = \mathbf{10.88}$
  • $\mathbf{w}_2 = [0.8(3.2), 0.2(0.4)]^T = \mathbf{[2.56, 0.08]^T} \implies f(\mathbf{w}_2) = 2.56^2 + 4(0.08^2) = \mathbf{6.5792}$
3. Geometric Insight: Loss dropped by $80\%$ across 2 steps. The steep $y$-axis shrunk $4\times$ faster than the shallow $x$-axis, illustrating why momentum and adaptive optimizers (Adam) are crucial in deep learning!
