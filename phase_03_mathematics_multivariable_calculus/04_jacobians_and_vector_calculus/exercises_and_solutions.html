Problem 1: Softmax Jacobian & Zero Column-Sum Invariant

Medium

🧒 The Context: Maya's 3-class animal classifier outputs probabilities $\mathbf{p} = [0.50, 0.30, 0.20]^T$.

  1. Compute the 9 entries of the $3 \times 3$ Softmax Jacobian matrix $J$.
  2. Sum the entries in each column.
  3. Explain the deep mathematical reason why every column sums to zero.
Click to view solution for Problem 1
1. Jacobian Entries:
  • $J_{11} = 0.50(1 - 0.50) = \mathbf{0.25}$,   $J_{22} = 0.30(1 - 0.30) = \mathbf{0.21}$,   $J_{33} = 0.20(1 - 0.20) = \mathbf{0.16}$
  • $J_{12} = J_{21} = -(0.50)(0.30) = \mathbf{-0.15}$
  • $J_{13} = J_{31} = -(0.50)(0.20) = \mathbf{-0.10}$
  • $J_{23} = J_{32} = -(0.30)(0.20) = \mathbf{-0.06}$
$$J = \begin{bmatrix} +0.25 & -0.15 & -0.10 \\ -0.15 & +0.21 & -0.06 \\ -0.10 & -0.06 & +0.16 \end{bmatrix}$$
2. Column Sums: Col 1: $+0.25 - 0.15 - 0.10 = \mathbf{0.00}$;   Col 2: $-0.15 + 0.21 - 0.06 = \mathbf{0.00}$;   Col 3: $-0.10 - 0.06 + 0.16 = \mathbf{0.00}$.
3. Mathematical Reason: Since $\sum_{i=1}^K p_i = 1.0$ (constant sum), $\frac{\partial}{\partial z_j}(\sum p_i) = \sum J_{ij} = 0$. Total probability is strictly conserved!

Problem 2: Softmax + Cross-Entropy Step-by-Step Gradient Collapse

Medium

🧒 The Context: For $\mathbf{p} = [0.50, 0.30, 0.20]^T$ and true label $\mathbf{y} = [0, 1, 0]^T$ (Cat):

  1. Compute upstream gradient $\mathbf{v} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{p}}$.
  2. Evaluate Vector-Jacobian Product $\nabla_{\mathbf{z}} \mathcal{L} = \mathbf{v} J$.
  3. Verify match with collapsed formula $\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{y}$.
Click to view solution for Problem 2
1. Upstream Gradient: $\mathbf{v} = [0, -1/p_2, 0] = [0, -1/0.30, 0]$.
2. Vector-Jacobian Product: $$\mathbf{v} J = [0, -3.333, 0] \begin{bmatrix} +0.25 & -0.15 & -0.10 \\ -0.15 & +0.21 & -0.06 \\ -0.10 & -0.06 & +0.16 \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{[+0.50, -0.70, +0.20]}^T$$
3. Direct Verification: $\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{y} = [0.50, 0.30, 0.20]^T - [0, 1, 0]^T = \mathbf{[+0.50, -0.70, +0.20]}^T$ ✅!

Problem 3: 2D Coordinate Transformation Jacobian & Determinant

Challenging

🧒 The Context: Normalizing flow mapping $y_1 = x_1^2 + x_2$ and $y_2 = 3x_1 + 2x_2$.

  1. Compute $2 \times 2$ Jacobian matrix $J(\mathbf{x})$.
  2. Compute determinant $\det(J(\mathbf{x}))$.
  3. Evaluate at $\mathbf{x} = [2, 1]^T$.
  4. Explain the physical expansion factor on a noise patch of area $0.02$.
Click to view solution for Problem 3
1. Jacobian Matrix: $J = \begin{bmatrix} 2x_1 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$.
2. Determinant Formula: $\det(J) = (2x_1)(2) - (1)(3) = \mathbf{4x_1 - 3}$.
3. Evaluation at $[2, 1]^T$: $J(2, 1) = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \implies \det(J) = 8 - 3 = \mathbf{5}$.
4. Area Expansion Factor: $\text{New Area} = |\det(J)| \times \text{Old Area} = 5 \times 0.02 = \mathbf{0.10}$. In normalizing flows, probability density drops by factor $5$ so total probability remains conserved at 1.0!
