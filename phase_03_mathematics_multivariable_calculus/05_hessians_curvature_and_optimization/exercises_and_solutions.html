Problem 1: Critical Point Discovery & Hessian Classification

Medium

🧒 The Context: Maya and Barnaby explore $f(x, y) = x^3 - 3x + y^2$.

  1. Find gradient $\nabla f(x, y)$.
  2. Solve $\nabla f = \mathbf{0}$ for all critical points.
  3. Compute Hessian $H(x, y)$.
  4. Classify each point using eigenvalues.
Click to view solution for Problem 1
1. Gradient: $\nabla f = [3x^2 - 3, 2y]^T$.
2. Critical Points: $3x^2 - 3 = 0 \implies x = \pm 1$;   $2y = 0 \implies y = 0$. Points are $A = (+1, 0)$ and $B = (-1, 0)$.
3. Hessian Matrix: $H(x, y) = \begin{bmatrix} 6x & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$.
4. Classification:
  • $H(1, 0) = \begin{bmatrix} 6 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \implies \lambda_1 = 6, \lambda_2 = 2 > 0 \implies$ Local Minimum (Soup Bowl).
  • $H(-1, 0) = \begin{bmatrix} -6 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \implies \lambda_1 = -6, \lambda_2 = 2 \implies$ Saddle Point (Pringle Chip).

Problem 2: Hand-Crafted Newton Optimization Step

Medium

🧒 The Context: Minimizing quadratic bowl $f(x, y) = 2x^2 + 8y^2$ from $\mathbf{w}_0 = [4.0, 2.0]^T$.

  1. Find $\nabla f(\mathbf{w}_0)$.
  2. Compute Hessian $H$ and $H^{-1}$.
  3. Execute Newton's step: $\mathbf{w}_1 = \mathbf{w}_0 - H^{-1} \nabla f(\mathbf{w}_0)$.
  4. Show it converges to $[0, 0]^T$ in exactly 1 step!
Click to view solution for Problem 2
1. Gradient: $\nabla f(4, 2) = [4(4), 16(2)]^T = \begin{bmatrix} 16.0 \\ 32.0 \end{bmatrix}$.
2. Hessian & Inverse: $H = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 16 \end{bmatrix} \implies H^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.0625 \end{bmatrix}$.
3. Newton's Step: $$\mathbf{w}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 4.0 \\ 2.0 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.0625 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 16.0 \\ 32.0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4.0 \\ 2.0 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 4.0 \\ 2.0 \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{\begin{bmatrix} 0.0 \\ 0.0 \end{bmatrix}}$$ Exact convergence in 1 step!

Problem 3: Condition Number & Stability Limit

Challenging

🧒 The Context: For ravine $f(x, y) = \frac{1}{2}x^2 + 50y^2$:

  1. Compute condition number $\kappa(H)$.
  2. Find maximum stable learning rate $\eta_{\max} = 2 / \lambda_{\max}$.
  3. Explain why $\eta = 0.025$ causes exponential blowup to `inf`.
Click to view solution for Problem 3
1. Condition Number: $H = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 100 \end{bmatrix} \implies \kappa(H) = \frac{100}{1} = \mathbf{100.0}$.
2. Stability Threshold: $\eta_{\max} = \frac{2}{\lambda_{\max}} = \frac{2}{100} = \mathbf{0.020}$.
3. Blowup Mechanism: For $\eta = 0.025$, the update factor along $y$ is $1 - \eta \lambda = 1 - 2.5 = \mathbf{-1.5}$. Because $|-1.5| > 1$, errors oscillate and grow exponentially every step, exploding to infinity!
