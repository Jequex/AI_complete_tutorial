Topic 3.1: Functions, Limits, Continuity & Derivatives

Calculus is the mathematical steering wheel of modern AI. When an AI misclassifies an image or mispredicts text, it uses derivatives to determine: "If I tweak this parameter by a tiny hair, will error go up or down, and by how much?"

🧒 1. The 12-Year-Old Analogy: Maya's Roller Coaster & The Super-Microscope

Imagine 12-year-old Maya and Timmy visiting an amusement park to ride the legendary "Hyper-Drop" roller coaster:

  • The Macro View: From the ground, the coaster tracks twist, loop, and drop unpredictably. Trying to describe the steepness of the entire ride with a single number is impossible.
  • The Super-Microscope: Maya takes out a magical pocket microscope that zooms in $50{,}000\times$ on point $P$. At that extreme zoom level, the curving rail no longer looks curved—it looks like a perfectly straight, flat ramp!
  • The Speedometer: The slope of that straight line is the derivative $\frac{df}{dx}$! In AI, neural networks use this exact slope microscope to adjust internal weights one tiny nudge at a time.

3. Limits & Continuity

To avoid dividing by zero ($\frac{0}{0}$ crash), we define the derivative as a limit:

$$\underbrace{\frac{df}{dx}}_{\text{Instantaneous slope}} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{\overbrace{f(x + h)}^{\text{Height after nudge}} - \overbrace{f(x)}^{\text{Starting height}}}{\underbrace{h}_{\text{Width of nudge}}}$$

🧒 What the math is actually doing: We evaluate the average slope over tiny intervals $h = 0.1, 0.01, 0.0001$. As $h \to 0$, the slope stabilizes onto the tangent line slope.

4. Fundamental Differentiation Rules

Power Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = n x^{n-1}$

First-Principles Proof for $f(x) = x^2$:

$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(x + h)^2 - x^2}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{x^2 + 2xh + h^2 - x^2}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} (2x + h) = \mathbf{2x}$$

Concrete Walkthrough at $x = 3$: Analytical slope is $2(3) = 6.0$. A finite step $h = 0.01$ gives $\frac{(3.01)^2 - 3^2}{0.01} = \frac{9.0601 - 9.0}{0.01} = 6.01 \approx 6.0$.

Product Rule: $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$

🧒 Expanding Carpet Analogy: A carpet of width $u$ and height $v$ has area $A = uv$. Nudging both dimensions adds two rectangular strips ($v \Delta u$ and $u \Delta v$) plus a microscopic corner ($\Delta u \Delta v$) that vanishes as $\Delta \to 0$.

5. Deep Learning Activation Function Derivatives

For the Sigmoid function $\sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}$, the derivative simplifies to:

$$\mathbf{\sigma'(x) = \sigma(x) \cdot (1 - \sigma(x))}$$
🚀 AI Speed Secret: If a neuron caches its forward output $y = 0.70$, backprop computes $dy = 0.70 \times (1 - 0.70) = \mathbf{0.21}$ with zero slow exponential calculations!

6. Numerical Gradient Checking: Forward vs Central Difference

  • Forward Difference: $\frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$   [Error: $\mathcal{O}(h)$]
  • Central Difference: $\frac{f(x+h) - f(x-h)}{2h}$   [Error: $\mathcal{O}(h^2)$ — 100,000x more accurate!]
