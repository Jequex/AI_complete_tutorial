2. The Limit Definition of the Derivative

From Secant to Tangent Line

The derivative measures the slope of the curve when zoomed in under a microscope:

$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}$$

Sensitivity Interpretation: If you nudge $x$ by a tiny hair $\Delta x$, the output changes by $\Delta f \approx f'(x) \cdot \Delta x$.

3. The Sigmoid Activation Derivative

In neural networks, the Sigmoid activation has a uniquely elegant derivative:

$$\sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}} \implies \mathbf{\sigma'(x) = \sigma(x) \cdot (1 - \sigma(x))}$$

Computational Efficiency in Deep Learning:
If a neuron cached forward output $y = \sigma(x)$, backpropagation computes the gradient without evaluating exponentials: $$dy = y \cdot (1 - y)$$

4. Numerical Differentiation: Finite Differences

To verify that analytical gradient code in PyTorch is free of bugs, we perform numerical gradient checks:

  • Forward Difference: $\frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h} \quad (\text{Error: } \mathcal{O}(h))$
  • Central Difference: $\frac{f(x + h) - f(x - h)}{2h} \quad (\text{Error: } \mathcal{O}(h^2))$

Central difference cancels first-order error terms, yielding $100{,}000\times$ higher precision for $h = 10^{-5}$!
