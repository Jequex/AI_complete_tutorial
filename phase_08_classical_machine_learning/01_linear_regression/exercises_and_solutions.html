Exercise 1: Manual Closed-Form Normal Equation Calculation

Given 3 observations: $(x_1, y_1) = (1, 2)$, $(x_2, y_2) = (2, 5)$, $(x_3, y_3) = (3, 7)$.

Compute $X^T X$, $X^T y$, invert $(X^T X)$, and solve for the optimal weights $w^* = [w_0, w_1]^T$.

View Complete Solution

1. Formulate Matrices:

$$X = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}, \quad y = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix}$$

2. Compute Products:

$$X^T X = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 6 \\ 6 & 14 \end{bmatrix}, \quad X^T y = \begin{bmatrix} 14 \\ 33 \end{bmatrix}$$

3. Matrix Inversion:

$$\det(X^T X) = (3)(14) - (6)^2 = 42 - 36 = 6$$ $$(X^T X)^{-1} = \frac{1}{6} \begin{bmatrix} 14 & -6 \\ -6 & 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 7/3 & -1 \\ -1 & 1/2 \end{bmatrix}$$

4. Solve for $w^*$:

$$w^* = \begin{bmatrix} 7/3 & -1 \\ -1 & 1/2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 14 \\ 33 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 98/3 - 33 \\ -14 + 33/2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -1/3 \\ 5/2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -0.3333 \\ 2.5000 \end{bmatrix}$$

Final line: $\hat{y} = 2.5 x - 0.3333$.

Exercise 2: Single-Step Gradient Descent Update

Using the dataset above with initial parameters $w_0 = [0, 0]^T$ and learning rate $\eta = 0.05$, compute predictions, residuals, the gradient vector $\nabla_w J$, and update $w_1$.

View Complete Solution

Initial Predictions: $\hat{y}_0 = [0, 0, 0]^T$.

Residuals: $e_0 = \hat{y}_0 - y = [-2, -5, -7]^T$.

Gradient:

$$\nabla_w J(w_0) = \frac{1}{3} X^T e_0 = \frac{1}{3} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ -5 \\ -7 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -14/3 \\ -33/3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -4.667 \\ -11.0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Parameter Update:

$$w_1 = w_0 - 0.05 \begin{bmatrix} -4.667 \\ -11.0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} +0.2333 \\ +0.5500 \end{bmatrix}$$

Exercise 3: Residual Orthogonality Proof

Prove that for any linear regression solved with the Normal Equation, the residual vector $e = y - Xw^*$ satisfies $X^T e = \mathbf{0}$, and that the sum of residuals $\sum e_i = 0$ if an intercept is present.

View Complete Solution

$$X^T e = X^T (y - X (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y) = X^T y - (X^T X)(X^T X)^{-1} X^T y = X^T y - X^T y = \mathbf{0}$$

Since the first column of $X$ is the column of ones $\mathbf{1}$, the first element of the vector $X^T e$ is $\mathbf{1}^T e = \sum_{i=1}^N e_i = 0$. $\blacksquare$

Exercise 4: Big-O FLOPs Scaling Benchmark

For $N = 10^7$ and $d = 1000$, contrast the arithmetic operations required for the Normal Equation vs. 20 epochs of Gradient Descent.

View Complete Solution
  • Normal Equation: $X^T X$ requires $N d^2 = 10^7 \cdot 10^6 = 10^{13}$ FLOPs. Inversion takes $\frac{1}{3} d^3 \approx 3.3 \times 10^8$ FLOPs. Total $\approx 10^{13}$ FLOPs.
  • Gradient Descent (20 epochs): $20 \times (2 N d) = 40 \times 10^7 \times 10^3 = 4 \times 10^{11}$ FLOPs.

Gradient Descent is 25 times faster and uses minimal memory.
