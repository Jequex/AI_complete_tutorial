Problem 1: Manual Forward and Backward Pass on XOR

Consider an MLP ($2 \to 2 \to 1$) with Tanh hidden activation and Sigmoid output, trained on sample $\mathbf{x} = [1, 0]^T$ with target $y = 1$:

$$W^{[1]} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.5 \\ -0.5 & -0.5 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b}^{[1]} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad W^{[2]} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad b^{[2]} = 0$$
  1. Compute $\mathbf{z}^{[1]}$, $\mathbf{a}^{[1]}$, $z^{[2]}$, and prediction $\hat{y}$.
  2. Compute output error $\delta^{[2]}$ and backpropagated hidden error $\boldsymbol{\delta}^{[1]}$.
  3. Compute parameter gradients $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[2]}}$ and $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[1]}}$.
View Complete Solution

1. Forward Pass:

$$\mathbf{z}^{[1]} = [0.5, -0.5]^T \implies \mathbf{a}^{[1]} \approx [0.4621, -0.4621]^T$$ $$z^{[2]} = 1(0.4621) + 1(-0.4621) = 0 \implies \hat{y} = \sigma(0) = 0.5$$

2. Error Signals:

$$\delta^{[2]} = \hat{y} - y = 0.5 - 1.0 = -0.5$$ $$\boldsymbol{\delta}^{[1]} = (\delta^{[2]} W^{[2]}) \odot (1 - (\mathbf{a}^{[1]})^2) = [-0.5, -0.5] \times 0.7864 \approx [-0.3932, -0.3932]^T$$

3. Parameter Gradients:

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[2]}} = \delta^{[2]} (\mathbf{a}^{[1]})^T \approx [-0.2311, 0.2311]$$ $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W^{[1]}} = \boldsymbol{\delta}^{[1]} \mathbf{x}^T \approx \begin{bmatrix} -0.3932 & 0 \\ -0.3932 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem 2: Derivation of Xavier / Glorot Initialization

Let $z = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{in}}} w_i x_i$ with independent zero-mean weights and inputs.

  1. Prove that $\operatorname{Var}(z) = n_{\text{in}} \operatorname{Var}(w) \operatorname{Var}(x)$.
  2. Derive the variance condition for forward stability and explain the harmonic mean compromise $\operatorname{Var}(w) = \frac{2}{n_{\text{in}} + n_{\text{out}}}$.
View Complete Solution

1. Variance derivation:

$$\operatorname{Var}(z) = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{in}}} \operatorname{Var}(w_i x_i) = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{in}}} \mathbb{E}[w_i^2]\mathbb{E}[x_i^2] = n_{\text{in}} \operatorname{Var}(w) \operatorname{Var}(x)$$

2. Stability compromise:

Forward pass stability requires $\operatorname{Var}(w) = 1 / n_{\text{in}}$. Backward pass stability requires $\operatorname{Var}(w) = 1 / n_{\text{out}}$. Taking the harmonic mean yields $\operatorname{Var}(w) = \frac{2}{n_{\text{in}} + n_{\text{out}}}$, giving uniform bounds $\pm \sqrt{\frac{6}{n_{\text{in}} + n_{\text{out}}}}$.

Problem 3: Hidden Representation Geometry in XOR

Let hidden neurons compute step functions $h_1 = \Theta(x_1 + x_2 - 0.5)$ and $h_2 = \Theta(x_1 + x_2 - 1.5)$.

  1. Map the four XOR input points into the 2D hidden space $(h_1, h_2)$.
  2. Prove that the points are linearly separable in this hidden space.
View Complete Solution

1. Coordinate mapping:

  • $(0, 0) \to (0, 0)$ (Class 0)
  • $(0, 1) \to (1, 0)$ (Class 1)
  • $(1, 0) \to (1, 0)$ (Class 1)
  • $(1, 1) \to (1, 1)$ (Class 0)

2. Linear separability:

The decision rule $h_1 - 2h_2 - 0.5 > 0$ yields $+0.5$ for $(1, 0)$ and $-0.5$ or $-1.5$ for $(0, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$. The hidden layer folds the space so the alternating corners collapse into a single cluster easily separated by a straight line.
