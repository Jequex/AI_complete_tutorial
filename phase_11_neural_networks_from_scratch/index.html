Neural Networks From Scratch

From artificial neurons and batch vectorization to vanishing gradients, cross-entropy cancellation, and building an end-to-end Multi-Layer Perceptron in pure Python.

01. Forward Propagation & Neural Network Architectures

The artificial neuron ($z = \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x} + b$), biological analogy vs mathematical reality, multi-layer feedforward architecture, batch vectorization ($Z^{[l]} = A^{[l-1]} W^{[l]T} + b^{[l]}$), tensor shape bookkeeping, and the Universal Approximation Theorem.

02. Activation Functions & The Vanishing Gradient Problem

Mathematical proof of linear collapse ($W_{\text{eff}} \mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}_{\text{eff}}$), comprehensive activation taxonomy (Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU, LeakyReLU, GELU, SiLU/SwiGLU), first derivatives, the exponential vanishing gradient problem ($0.25^L \to 0$), and the Dead ReLU pathology.

03. Loss Functions & Analytical Derivatives

Mean Squared Error (MSE), Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE), Softmax Categorical Cross-Entropy, analytical gradient derivations, the universal error cancellation ($\hat{\mathbf{y}} - \mathbf{y}$), Focal Loss for class imbalance, and non-convex loss landscape topology.

04. Multilayer Perceptrons From Scratch

Complete Multi-Layer Perceptron implementation in pure Python. Xavier/Glorot initialization, cached forward propagation, analytical backpropagation via chain rule, overcoming the historic Minsky & Papert XOR barrier ($50\% \to 100\%$ accuracy), and non-linear concentric circles classification.
