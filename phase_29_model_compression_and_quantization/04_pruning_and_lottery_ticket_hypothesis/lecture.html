Topic 29.4: Pruning & The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis

Sparsity Taxonomies, NVIDIA 2:4 Ampere Architecture, Iterative Magnitude Pruning, and Weight Rewinding

1. 12-Year-Old Intuition: The Basketball Tryout Analogy

Imagine 100 kids show up for high school basketball tryouts (the dense network). During a week of intense drills, 15 standout players emerge with natural chemistry, quick reflexes, and court vision. The coach cuts the other 85 players and keeps only the 15 stars (pruned to 85% sparsity). This 15-player squad goes on to win the state championship!

In 2018, Frankle & Carbin asked: "Could we have picked these 15 kids on Day 1?"

They discovered: If you take those exact 15 kids, reset them back to their Day 1 starting skills (rewinding $\theta_0$), and train them alone, they win the championship again! But if you keep the same roster spots and invite 15 random kids off the street ($\theta_0'$), they fail miserably. Inside every dense network, a lucky winning ticket was already present at initialization.

2. Mathematical Symbol Decoder

$s \in [0, 1)$ Sparsity Ratio $[0, 1)$ Percentage of total network weights set to zero: $1 - \|\mathbf{m}\|_0 / D$.
$N:M$ Semi-Structured Sparsity Discrete ratio NVIDIA Ampere constraint: exactly $N$ out of every $M$ contiguous weights are non-zero (2:4).

3. Pruning Taxonomy

1. Unstructured Pruning (Fine-Grained Magnitude Pruning)

Removes individual scalar weights with $|\theta_i| < \tau$. Reaches extreme theoretical sparsity (80–90%), but creates irregular memory patterns that do not accelerate standard dense GPU matrix engines without specialized sparse libraries.

2. Structured Pruning (Coarse-Grained Channel/Head Pruning)

Removes entire channels, filters, or attention heads, physically shrinking matrix dimensions $(M \times K) \to (M \times K')$. Provides immediate $2\times$ to $4\times$ throughput speedup on any off-the-shelf CPU or GPU, but hurts expressivity past 50% sparsity.

3. Semi-Structured 2:4 Sparsity (NVIDIA Ampere/Hopper)

Mandates that in every contiguous block of 4 weights across a row, exactly 2 must be zero. NVIDIA Sparse Tensor Cores execute these at $2\times$ native math throughput with zero index storage overhead.

4. The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis & Weight Rewinding

The Core Hypothesis (Frankle & Carbin, 2018)

$$\theta_{\text{ticket}} = \mathbf{m} \odot \theta_0$$

When trained in isolation, the winning subnetwork matches the full dense model's test accuracy in comparable training steps. The winning ticket requires BOTH the mask $\mathbf{m}$ and the initial coordinates $\theta_0$.

The Random Control Proof

$$\theta_{\text{random}} = \mathbf{m} \odot \theta_0'$$

Reinitializing with fresh random weights $\theta_0'$ on the identical mask $\mathbf{m}$ causes substantial accuracy collapse, proving that random sparsity alone is insufficient without the inductive bias of initial weights.
