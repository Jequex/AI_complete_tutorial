Model Compression & Quantization

From 32-bit continuous tensors to sub-4-bit discrete representations: Uniform Quantization, PTQ vs QAT, GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant, Structured Pruning, and Knowledge Distillation.

01. Uniform Quantization Mechanics

Real-to-integer mappings: Uniform affine (asymmetric) quantization $q = \text{clip}\left(\left\lfloor \frac{x}{s} \right\rceil + z, q_{\min}, q_{\max}\right)$, symmetric zero-point elimination ($z = 0$, $s = \frac{\|x\|_\infty}{2^{b-1}-1}$), and exact zero preservation. Detailed mathematical derivation of Bennett's quantization noise model ($\sigma_e^2 = \frac{s^2}{12}$) and Signal-to-Quantization-Noise Ratio ($\text{SQNR} \approx 6.02b + 1.76\text{ dB}$).

02. Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) vs. Quantization-Aware Training (QAT)

PTQ calibration strategies: MinMax vs Percentile vs KL-divergence (NVIDIA TensorRT relative entropy minimization). QAT forward pass with fake quantization, the zero-derivative rounding barrier ($\frac{d\lfloor x \rceil}{dx} = 0 \text{ a.e.}$), the Straight-Through Estimator (STE, Bengio 2013), and Learned Step Size Quantization (LSQ, Esser 2020).

03. Advanced Quantization: GPTQ, AWQ & SmoothQuant

Second-order Taylor curvature $\Delta \mathcal{L} \approx \frac{1}{2} \Delta \mathbf{w}^\top \mathbf{H} \Delta \mathbf{w}$ with layer Hessian $\mathbf{H} = 2 \mathbf{X}^\top \mathbf{X}$. Optimal Brain Surgeon (OBS) and GPTQ inverse Hessian greedy compensation $\Delta \mathbf{w} = -\frac{w_q - \hat{w}_q}{[\mathbf{H}^{-1}]_{qq}} \mathbf{H}^{-1}_{:, q}$. Activation-aware Weight Quantization (AWQ) protecting the top 1% salient weights. SmoothQuant channel-wise outlier migration $\mathbf{S} = \text{diag}(s_j)$.

04. Pruning & The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis

Pruning taxonomy: fine-grained unstructured magnitude pruning vs coarse-grained structured channel/head pruning vs NVIDIA Ampere 2:4 semi-structured sparsity. Iterative Magnitude Pruning (IMP). The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis (Frankle & Carbin, 2018): winning subnetworks $\mathbf{m} \odot \theta_0$ that train to dense accuracy in isolation, and weight rewinding protocols.

05. Knowledge Distillation

Teacher-Student paradigm (Geoffrey Hinton et al., 2015). Soft targets and "dark knowledge". Temperature-scaled softmax $p_i(T) = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_j \exp(z_j / T)}$. Mathematical derivation of the high-temperature limit proving gradient equivalence to matching unnormalized logits ($\nabla_{z_i^s} \mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}} \approx \frac{1}{C T^2}(z_i^s - z_i^t)$). Composite loss $\mathcal{L} = (1 - \alpha)\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}} + \alpha T^2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}$.
