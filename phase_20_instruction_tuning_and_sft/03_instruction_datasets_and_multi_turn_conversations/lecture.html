Topic 20.3: Instruction Datasets, Multi-Turn Conversations & Function Calling

Dataset Engineering Pipelines, Multi-Turn Loss Alignment, and Agentic Tool Invocation

Executive Summary

Instruction data is the lifeblood of alignment. Synthetic generation frameworks such as Self-Instruct and Evol-Instruct generate diverse, high-complexity reasoning datasets from small initial seeds. When modeling multi-turn dialogues and function-calling workflows, all conversational turns are compiled into a unified context sequence with precise loss boundaries: system instructions, user turns, and external tool responses are masked out (-100), while assistant thoughts, tool call queries, and final answers are actively supervised.

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Rehearsal Script for a Movie Actor

Imagine you are an actor rehearsing for a superhero movie:

  • The script contains scene directions (EXT. WAREHOUSE - RAINY NIGHT), villain lines, sound effects ([Lightning strikes]), and your hero lines ("Stop right there!").
  • When you rehearse, you read the whole scene to understand the story. But you only practice saying your lines! You don't practice reciting the stage directions, and you don't practice making thunder noises!

In AI fine-tuning: user queries and system prompts are the other actors and scene directions; external tool responses are the sound effects; the LLM is the actor. The model conditions on everything, but is graded only on its own lines!

Instruction Dataset Engineering Pipelines

Self-Instruct vs. Evol-Instruct

  • Self-Instruct: Bootstraps tasks from 8 in-context demonstrations, classifies input requirement, synthesizes outputs, and filters using ROUGE-L similarity.
  • Evol-Instruct: Deepens complexity via in-depth operators (adding constraints, deepening reasoning steps, concretizing inputs) and in-breadth operators (topic mutation).

Tool Use & Function Calling Masking Rules

[SYSTEM: Tools Schema]               ──> MASKED (-100)
[USER: "Check weather in Tokyo"]     ──> MASKED (-100)
[ASSISTANT: <tool_call>...</tool_call>] ──> SUPERVISED (Model learns tool generation)
[TOOL_OUTPUT: {"temp": 22, "rain": 0}] ──> MASKED (-100: Produced by environment, not LLM!)
[ASSISTANT: "Tokyo is 22°C with no rain."] ──> SUPERVISED (Model learns synthesis)

Sequence Packing

To eliminate wasted compute on padding tokens during distributed training, multiple conversations are concatenated into fixed-length windows. Segment IDs and block-diagonal causal attention masks prevent cross-dialogue attention leakage.
