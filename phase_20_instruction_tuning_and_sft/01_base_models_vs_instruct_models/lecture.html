Topic 20.1: Base Models vs. Instruct Models & Chat Templates

The Fundamental Paradigm Shift from Internet Auto-Completer to Aligned Assistant

The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Walking Encyclopedia vs. The Helpful Teacher

Imagine two characters in a school library:

  • The Walking Encyclopedia (The Base Model): Has memorized every book, forum post, and magazine in existence. But when you ask it: "What is the capital of France?", it doesn't say "Paris!" Instead, because it has read thousands of geography tests, it continues reciting: "What is the capital of Spain? What is the capital of Germany? Time limit: 15 minutes." It isn't dumb—it simply assumes its only job is reading the next line of the book!
  • The Helpful Teacher (The Instruct Model): Possesses the exact same encyclopedic memory, but went through teacher training (Instruction Tuning). The teacher recognizes the social contract: you are asking for help, and they should answer directly: "The capital of France is Paris."

The Triad of Alignment: HHH

  • Helpfulness: Following user intent, respecting explicit positive/negative constraints, executing complex reasoning, and avoiding unhelpful evasiveness.
  • Honesty: Generating factually accurate content, expressing calibrated uncertainty, and avoiding hallucinations or fabricated citations.
  • Harmlessness: Gracefully refusing malicious requests (e.g. malware synthesis, weapons manufacturing) while distinguishing defensive/educational inquiries.

Prompt Formats & Chat Templates

1. Stanford Alpaca Format (Plaintext Delimiters)

Below is an instruction that describes a task...

### Instruction:
Calculate the area of a circle with radius 5.

### Response:
The area of a circle is A = π * r^2. For r = 5, A = 25π ≈ 78.54.

Vulnerability: Plaintext tokens like ### Response: can be typed by users to stage prompt injections and hijack instructions.

2. OpenAI ChatML Format (Special Control Tokens)

<|im_start|>system
You are a helpful assistant.<|im_end|>
<|im_start|>user
What causes ocean tides?<|im_end|>
<|im_start|>assistant
Ocean tides are primarily caused by the gravitational pull of the Moon and Sun.<|im_end|>

3. Meta LLaMA 3 Header Format

<|begin_of_text|><|start_header_id|>system<|end_header_id|>

You are an expert AI assistant.<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>user<|end_header_id|>

How do I reverse a list in Python?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

You can use slicing: my_list[::-1]<|eot_id|>

Generation Prompt Mechanics

When prompting the model to produce an answer, we append an unclosed assistant header: <|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>\n\n (LLaMA 3) or <|im_start|>assistant\n (ChatML). The autoregressive loop predicts tokens until emitting the end-of-turn token <|eot_id|> or <|im_end|>, which instructs the runtime to halt generation.
