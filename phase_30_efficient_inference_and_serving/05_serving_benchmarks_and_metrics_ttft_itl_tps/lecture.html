Topic 30.5

Serving Benchmarks & Telemetry: TTFT, ITL, TPS

Time to First Token, Inter-Token Latency, System vs. User Throughput, Little's Law, and Open-Loop Poisson load benchmarking.

1. The 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine dining at an exclusive sushi restaurant:

  • Time to First Token (TTFT): How long you sit at the counter before the chef hands you your very first piece of sashimi. If this takes 30 minutes, you leave unhappy.
  • Inter-Token Latency (ITL): The rhythmic delay between consecutive pieces of sushi. Once eating starts, you want a new piece every 20 seconds. If the chef drops one piece and then vanishes for 10 minutes, the experience feels broken.
  • System Tokens Per Second (System TPS): The restaurant owner's view: total pieces of sushi served per hour across all 40 seats. The owner wants high throughput; you want low latency!

2. Telemetry KPI Definitions

Metric Formal Definition Target Interactive SLA Primary Bottleneck
$\text{TTFT}$ $T_{\text{queue}} + T_{\text{prefill}}$ $< 200\text{--}400 \text{ ms}$ Prompt length (GEMM compute) & request queue buffering
$\text{ITL}$ (or $\text{TPOT}$) $t_i - t_{i-1}$ $< 30\text{--}50 \text{ ms/token}$ HBM memory bandwidth (GEMV decode) & batch size
$\text{TPS}_{\text{user}}$ $1 / \text{ITL}$ $> 20\text{--}30 \text{ tok/s}$ Single-stream decode speed
$\text{TPS}_{\text{sys}}$ $\sum N_{\text{tokens}} / \Delta T$ Application-dependent GPU compute saturation & aggregate cluster concurrency

3. Queueing Dynamics & Little's Law

In steady-state request scheduling, Little's Law guarantees:

$$\boxed{L = \lambda \cdot W}$$

The Congestion Collapse Knee

Under Poisson arrival traffic with service capacity $\mu$ and load intensity $\rho = \lambda / \mu$:

$$W = \frac{1/\mu}{1 - \rho}, \qquad L_q = \frac{\rho^2}{1 - \rho}$$

When load exceeds $\rho > 0.85$, queues explode asymptotically, causing tail percentiles ($p90, p99$) to deteriorate by orders of magnitude even as throughput appears high.
