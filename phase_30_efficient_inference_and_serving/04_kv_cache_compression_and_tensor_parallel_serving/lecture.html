Topic 30.4

KV Cache Compression & Tensor Parallel Serving

Architectural head grouping (MQA/GQA), Heavy Hitter Oracle (H2O) eviction, and distributed Megatron KV cache sharding.

1. The 12-Year-Old Intuition

Imagine investigating a 1,000-page mystery. If you keep all 1,000 pages spread across your desk, you run out of space immediately.

  • Smart Pruning (H2O Eviction): You keep the first 4 pages (the suspect list & premise), the last 10 pages (what you just saw), and 20 critical clues in the middle. You toss the 966 pages of useless filler. You save 80% of desk space with 99% accuracy!
  • Distributed Sharding (Tensor Parallelism): If the case file is still too big, you hire 4 assistant detectives and divide the attention heads. Detective 1 tracks Motive, Detective 2 tracks Alibis, Detective 3 tracks Forensics, Detective 4 tracks Timelines. Each detective holds only 1/4 of the notes on their desk, and they whisper conclusions at each step via an All-Reduce.

3. The Heavy Hitter Oracle (H2O) & Attention Sinks

Softmax attention creates distinct patterns in long-context sequences:

The Tri-Partite Eviction Structure

  • Attention Sinks ($W_{\text{sink}} \approx 4$ tokens): Initial prompt tokens hold large residual softmax mass regardless of semantics. Evicting them causes perplexity to explode.
  • Recency Window ($W_{\text{recency}} \approx 64\text{--}128$): Local context essential for grammar and immediate coherence.
  • Heavy Hitters ($k_{\text{heavy}}$): Tokens with highest cumulative attention scores $\sum_{\tau} \sum_h A_{\tau, j}^{(h)}$.

Tokens outside these three sets are evicted, compressing the KV footprint by $60\text{--}80\%$ while maintaining $>95\%$ generation accuracy.

4. Tensor Parallel KV Cache Sharding

Under Megatron-LM Tensor Parallelism across $N_{\text{tp}}$ GPUs:

$$W_Q, W_K, W_V \in \text{Column Parallel} \implies \text{Attention Heads partitioned across GPUs}$$ $$\boxed{\text{Mem}_{\text{KV\_per\_GPU}} = \frac{4 \cdot n_{\text{layers}} \cdot H_{\text{kv}} \cdot d_{\text{head}} \cdot b \cdot s}{N_{\text{tp}}}}$$

Each GPU holds only $1 / N_{\text{tp}}$ of the total KV cache. An 8-GPU node provides $8\times$ the KV cache memory capacity.
