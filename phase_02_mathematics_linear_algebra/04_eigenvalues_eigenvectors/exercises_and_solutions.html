Problem 1: Full Eigendecomposition by Hand

For symmetric matrix $B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$, find eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ and eigenvectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2$.

Click to view solution $$\det(B - \lambda I) = (2 - \lambda)^2 - 1 = \lambda^2 - 4\lambda + 3 = 0 \implies \mathbf{\lambda_1 = 3, \quad \lambda_2 = 1}$$
  • For $\lambda_1 = 3$: $\mathbf{v}_1 = [1, 1]^T$
  • For $\lambda_2 = 1$: $\mathbf{v}_2 = [1, -1]^T$
  • Orthogonality check: $\mathbf{v}_1 \cdot \mathbf{v}_2 = (1)(1) + (1)(-1) = \mathbf{0}$ ✓

Problem 2: Fast Matrix Powers via Diagonalization

Compute $B^4 = Q \Lambda^4 Q^{-1}$ step-by-step.

Click to view solution $$\Lambda^4 = \begin{bmatrix} 3^4 & 0 \\ 0 & 1^4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 81 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$B^4 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 81 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.5 \\ 0.5 & -0.5 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 41 & 40 \\ 40 & 41 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem 3: RNN Gradient Dynamics

For $\mathbf{h}_t = \lambda^t \mathbf{h}_0$, compute scaling after $t = 100$ steps for $\lambda = 1.10$ and $\lambda = 0.90$.

Click to view solution
  • For $\lambda = 1.10$: $1.10^{100} \approx \mathbf{13,780.6}$ (Exploding gradient!)
  • For $\lambda = 0.90$: $0.90^{100} \approx \mathbf{0.00002656}$ (Vanishing gradient!)
