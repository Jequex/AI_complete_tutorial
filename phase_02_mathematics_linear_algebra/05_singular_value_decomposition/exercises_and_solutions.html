Practice step-by-step SVD derivations, low-rank image approximation, and pseudoinverse calculations.

Problem 1: Step-by-Step SVD of a Rank-1 Matrix

For $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$, find singular values $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$, vectors $\mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{v}_1$, and verify $A = \sigma_1 \mathbf{u}_1 \mathbf{v}_1^T$.

Click to view solution
  • $A^T A = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 10 \\ 10 & 20 \end{bmatrix} \implies \lambda_1 = 25, \lambda_2 = 0$
  • Singular values: $\sigma_1 = \sqrt{25} = \mathbf{5.0}, \quad \sigma_2 = \mathbf{0.0}$
  • $\mathbf{v}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} [1, 2]^T, \quad \mathbf{u}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} [1, 2]^T$
  • Verification: $5 \left(\frac{1}{5} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}\right) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix} = A$ ✓

Problem 2: Eckart-Young Low-Rank Approximation Error

A $100 \times 100$ matrix has singular values $\sigma_1 = 10, \sigma_2 = 6, \sigma_3 = 2, \sigma_4 = 1$. Compute $\|X\|_F$ and the energy retained by rank-2 approximation $X_2$.

Click to view solution $$\|X\|_F = \sqrt{100 + 36 + 4 + 1} = \sqrt{141} \approx \mathbf{11.874}$$ $$\|X - X_2\|_F = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5} \approx \mathbf{2.236}$$ $$\text{Energy Retained} = \frac{100 + 36}{141} \approx \mathbf{96.45\%}$$

Problem 3: Moore-Penrose Pseudoinverse ($A^+$)

For $A = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$, find $A^+$ and verify $A A^+ A = A$.

Click to view solution $$A^+ = \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.50 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$A A^+ A = A I_2 = A \quad \text{✓}$$
