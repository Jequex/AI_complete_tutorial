Topic 2.3: Vector Spaces, Span, Basis, and Rank

Linear combinations, spanning sets, matrix column spaces, null spaces, rank-nullity theorem, and LoRA.

1. 🧒 The 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Redundant Teammate & The Sandbox

🧒 The Island Game

Timmy can move East/West ($[1, 0]^T$), and Maya can move North/South ($[0, 1]^T$). Together, they can explore every square of a 2D island!

If Barnaby the puppy joins and can run Northeast ($[1, 1]^T$), he adds zero new territory because $[1, 1]^T = 1[1, 0]^T + 1[0, 1]^T$. Barnaby is linearly dependent (redundant)!

2. Rank-Nullity Theorem & Subspaces

🔬 Conservation of Dimensions
$$\text{Rank}(A) + \text{Nullity}(A) = n$$

🧒 What the math is actually doing: Every input dimension must either survive into the output column space $\mathcal{C}(A)$ or get squashed into the null space $\mathcal{N}(A)$.

For $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$, Col 2 is $2 \times$ Col 1. $\text{Rank} = 1$, and $\text{Nullity} = 1$ (the line $x_1 = -2x_2$ squashes to 0). $1 + 1 = 2$ dimensions!
