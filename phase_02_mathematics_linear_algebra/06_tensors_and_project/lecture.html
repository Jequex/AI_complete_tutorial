Topic 2.6: Tensors & Capstone Project

Multi-dimensional memory layouts, broadcasting mechanics, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

1. 🧒 The 12-Year-Old Analogy: From a Coin to a Shipping Container

🧒 The Dimensional Ladder

A scalar is a coin (0D). A vector is a ruler (1D). A matrix is a sheet of paper (2D). A 3D tensor is a book of color channels. A 4D tensor is a shipping container of books $(B \times C \times H \times W)$ sent in parallel to a GPU!

2. PCA: The Dinosaur Shadow Puppet

🔬 Finding the Optimal Projection Angle
$$C = \frac{1}{N - 1} \tilde{X}^T \tilde{X}, \qquad C \mathbf{v}_1 = \lambda_1 \mathbf{v}_1$$

🧒 What the math is actually doing: Imagine shining a flashlight on a 3D wire dinosaur model. PCA finds the exact angle that projects the largest, most informative 2D shadow onto the wall!
