2. Multivariate Gaussian in Generative AI

Why $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \Sigma)$ Shapes Generative Models

In high dimensions, the covariance matrix $\Sigma$ governs how variables stretch and correlate with each other:

  • Spherical ($\Sigma = \sigma^2 I$): Symmetrical, isotropic circular contours.
  • Diagonal ($\Sigma = \text{diag}(\sigma_1^2, \dots, \sigma_D^2)$): Axis-aligned ellipses with independent coordinates.
  • Full Covariance: Rotated ellipses capturing complex cross-feature dependencies.
The VAE Reparameterization Trick:
To backpropagate through stochastic Gaussian sampling: $$\mathbf{z} = \mu + \sigma \odot \epsilon \quad \text{where } \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$$ This separates stochastic noise $\epsilon$ from trainable parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma$!
