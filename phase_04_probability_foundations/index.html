Probability Foundations for AI

Probability is the mathematical logic of uncertainty. Machine learning models never deal in absolute certainties; they observe noisy evidence, quantify beliefs, and make decisions under incomplete information.

4.1 Probability Fundamentals & Bayes' Theorem Topic 4.1

Sample spaces, Kolmogorov's three axioms, conditional probability, statistical independence, Bayes' rule ($P(\theta|D)$), and the False Positive Paradox in medical screening.

4.2 Random Variables, PMF, PDF & CDF Topic 4.2

Discrete vs continuous uncertainty, Probability Mass Functions, the continuous PDF density paradox ($P(X=x)=0$), Cumulative Distribution Functions, and Inverse Transform Sampling.

4.3 Core Probability Distributions in AI Topic 4.3

Bernoulli, Binomial, Categorical token distributions, univariate Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, and Multivariate Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \Sigma)$ in VAE latent spaces and diffusion models.

4.4 Expectation, Variance & Covariance Topic 4.4

Center of mass, Linearity of Expectation ($\mathbb{E}[aX+bY]$), variance scaling, covariance $\text{Cov}(X, Y)$, Pearson correlation $\rho$, Batch Normalization, and He weight initialization.

The Law of Large Numbers, the Central Limit Theorem, Monte Carlo high-dimensional integration ($O(1/\sqrt{N})$), and LLM text generation decoding (Temperature, Top-K, and Top-P Nucleus).
