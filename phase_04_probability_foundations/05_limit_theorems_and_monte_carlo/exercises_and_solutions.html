Problem 1: CLT & Benchmark Test Suite Sample Sizing

Medium

A lab benchmarks model accuracy ($p \approx 0.80$). Using the Central Limit Theorem, calculate how many benchmark questions $n$ are required to guarantee a margin of error within $\pm 1\%$ ($E \le 0.01$) with $95\%$ confidence ($Z = 1.96$).

Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
1. Standard Error Formula: $$\text{SE}(\hat{p}) = \sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}$$
2. Margin of Error Inversion: $$E = 1.96 \sqrt{\frac{0.80 \times 0.20}{n}} \le 0.01 \implies n \ge \frac{1.96^2 \times 0.16}{0.0001} \approx \mathbf{6{,}147 \text{ questions}}$$ Evaluating models on small test sets (e.g. 100 questions) leaves wide margins of error ($\pm 10\%$).

Problem 2: Monte Carlo Integration for Gaussian Error Integral

Medium

Estimate $\int_0^1 e^{-x^2} dx$ using Monte Carlo with $N = 4$ uniform samples: $u = [0.2, 0.5, 0.8, 0.1]$.

Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
Hand Calculation: $$\hat{I}_4 = \frac{e^{-0.2^2} + e^{-0.5^2} + e^{-0.8^2} + e^{-0.1^2}}{4} = \frac{0.9608 + 0.7788 + 0.5273 + 0.9901}{4} \approx \mathbf{0.8142}$$ True integral is $\approx 0.7468$.

Problem 3: LLM Decoding by Hand (Temperature, Top-K & Top-P)

Challenging

Given raw logits $[3.0, 2.0, 1.0, 0.0]$ for {"apple", "banana", "carrot", "donut"}:

  1. Compute probabilities under standard Softmax ($T = 1.0$).
  2. Compute probabilities under high temperature ($T = 2.0$).
  3. Compute probabilities under Top-2 truncation.
  4. Compute probabilities under Nucleus Top-P ($P = 0.85$).
Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
1. Standard Softmax ($T = 1.0$): $$p = [64.39\%, 23.69\%, 8.71\%, 3.21\%]$$
2. High Temp ($T = 2.0$): Scaled logits: $[1.5, 1.0, 0.5, 0.0] \implies p = [45.51\%, 27.60\%, 16.74\%, 10.15\%]$
3. Top-2 Truncation: Keep {"apple", "banana"}, re-normalize: $p = [73.11\%, 26.89\%, 0\%, 0\%]$
4. Top-P ($P = 0.85$): Cumulative: Apple ($0.6439$) + Banana ($0.2369$) $= 0.8808 \ge 0.85$. Keeps {"apple", "banana"}, pruning carrot and donut! $$p_{\text{apple}} = 73.10\%, \quad p_{\text{banana}} = 26.90\%$$
