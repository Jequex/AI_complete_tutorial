Topic 4.5: Limit Theorems, Monte Carlo & LLM Sampling

Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, Monte Carlo, and Temperature / Nucleus Decoding

1. The Two Foundational Pillars: LLN and CLT

1. The Law of Large Numbers (LLN)

As sample size $n \to \infty$, the sample average $\bar{X}_n$ converges to the true expected value $\mu$:

$$\bar{X}_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i \xrightarrow{P} \mu$$

Why AI Works: Training loss on large empirical datasets converges to real-world population risk!

2. The Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

The sum or average of independent random variables from any arbitrary distribution converges to a Gaussian bell curve:

$$\frac{\bar{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$$

2. Monte Carlo Methods: Escaping the Curse of Dimensionality

The Monte Carlo Approximation:
$$\mathbb{E}_{x \sim p}[g(x)] = \int_{\mathbb{R}^D} g(x) p(x) \, dx \approx \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N g(x_i)$$ The error decreases at rate $\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{N})$, completely independent of the dimension $D$!

3. LLM Token Generation Strategies

Decoding Strategy Formula / Algorithm Behavior & Optimal AI Use Case
Greedy $y = \arg\max_k z_k$ Deterministic, highly repetitive, prone to loops
Temperature ($T$) $p_k = \frac{e^{z_k / T}}{\sum_j e^{z_j / T}}$ Low $T$: factual/coding; High $T$: creative writing
Top-$K$ Truncate to top $K$ probabilities Cuts off the bizarre long tail of garbage tokens
Top-$P$ (Nucleus) Keep smallest set with $\sum p_i \ge P$ Dynamically adjusts candidate pool based on model certainty
