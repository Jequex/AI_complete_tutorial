Problem 1: Kolmogorov Axiom Proofs & 3-Set Inclusion-Exclusion

Medium

Using only Kolmogorov's three axioms:

  1. Prove that $P(\emptyset) = 0$.
  2. Prove that $P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$.
  3. Derive the 3-Set Inclusion-Exclusion formula for $P(A \cup B \cup C)$.
Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
1. Proof that $P(\emptyset) = 0$: $$\Omega \cup \emptyset = \Omega \quad (\text{with } \Omega \cap \emptyset = \emptyset)$$ $$P(\Omega \cup \emptyset) = P(\Omega) + P(\emptyset) \implies 1 = 1 + P(\emptyset) \implies P(\emptyset) = 0$$
2. Proof that $P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$: $$A \cup A^c = \Omega \quad (\text{with } A \cap A^c = \emptyset)$$ $$P(A) + P(A^c) = P(\Omega) = 1 \implies P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$$
3. 3-Set Inclusion-Exclusion: $$P(A \cup B \cup C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C) - P(A \cap B) - P(A \cap C) - P(B \cap C) + P(A \cap B \cap C)$$

Problem 2: Bayesian Sequential Updating: The Two-Test Miracle

Medium

In a disease with prevalence $P(D) = 0.0001$, a test with 99% sensitivity and 1% false positive rate yielded an initial positive test: $P(D \mid \text{Test}_1 = +) \approx 0.98\%$.

Suppose the patient is retested with an independent test that also comes back positive. Compute the new updated posterior $P(D \mid \text{Test}_1 = +, \text{Test}_2 = +)$.

Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
1. Updated Prior: $$P(\text{Disease}) = 0.009804, \quad P(\text{Healthy}) = 0.990196$$
2. Updated Bayesian Calculation: $$\text{Numerator} = 0.99 \times 0.009804 \approx 0.009706$$ $$\text{Denominator} = (0.99 \times 0.009804) + (0.01 \times 0.990196) \approx 0.019608$$ $$P(D \mid +, +) = \frac{0.009706}{0.019608} \approx \mathbf{49.5\%}$$

Problem 3: Naive Bayes Classification & Laplace Smoothing

Challenging

Calculate by hand the probability of the message "buy cash prize" being SPAM given $P(\text{Spam}) = 0.60$, $P(\text{Ham}) = 0.40$, $V=100$, and Laplace (+1) smoothing.

Click to Reveal Step-by-Step Solution
Smoothed Likelihoods: $$\text{Spam: } P(\text{buy}) = \frac{41}{600}, \quad P(\text{cash}) = \frac{26}{600}, \quad P(\text{prize}) = \frac{36}{600}$$ $$\text{Ham: } P(\text{buy}) = \frac{11}{1100}, \quad P(\text{cash}) = \frac{3}{1100}, \quad P(\text{prize}) = \frac{2}{1100}$$
Posterior Scores: $$\text{Score}_{\text{spam}} \approx 0.60 \times 0.06833 \times 0.04333 \times 0.06000 \approx 1.066 \times 10^{-4}$$ $$\text{Score}_{\text{ham}} \approx 0.40 \times 0.01000 \times 0.00273 \times 0.00182 \approx 1.983 \times 10^{-8}$$ $$P(\text{Spam} \mid \text{message}) = \frac{1.066 \times 10^{-4}}{1.066 \times 10^{-4} + 1.983 \times 10^{-8}} \approx \mathbf{99.98\%}$$
