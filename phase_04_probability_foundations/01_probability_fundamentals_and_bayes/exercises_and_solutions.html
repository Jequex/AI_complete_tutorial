Problem 1: Kolmogorov Axiom Proofs & 3-Set Inclusion-Exclusion

Medium

🧒 The Context: Proving foundational probability theorems from Kolmogorov's 3 axioms ($P(A) \ge 0$, $P(\Omega) = 1$, additivity for disjoint sets).

  1. Prove $P(\emptyset) = 0$.
  2. Prove $P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$.
  3. Prove Monotonicity: $A \subseteq B \implies P(A) \le P(B)$.
  4. Derive 3-set Inclusion-Exclusion $P(A \cup B \cup C)$.
Click to view solution for Problem 1
1. Empty Set: $P(\Omega \cup \emptyset) = P(\Omega) + P(\emptyset) \implies 1 = 1 + P(\emptyset) \implies P(\emptyset) = \mathbf{0}$.
2. Complement: $P(A \cup A^c) = P(A) + P(A^c) \implies 1 = P(A) + P(A^c) \implies \mathbf{P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)}$.
3. Monotonicity: $B = A \cup (B \setminus A) \implies P(B) = P(A) + P(B \setminus A) \ge P(A)$ since probabilities are non-negative.
4. 3-Set Formula: $$P(A \cup B \cup C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C) - P(AB) - P(AC) - P(BC) + P(ABC)$$

Problem 2: Bayesian Sequential Updating: The Two-Test Miracle

Medium

🧒 The Context: After Barnaby tested positive on Test 1 ($P(\text{Sick}) = 0.09016$), Maya orders an independent Test 2 that also comes back positive.

  1. Using Test 1's posterior as the new prior, compute $P(\text{Sick} \mid \text{Test}_1^+, \text{Test}_2^+)$.
  2. Show how two consecutive tests eliminate the false positive trap.
Click to view solution for Problem 2
1. Setup New Evidence: Prior $P(\text{Sick}) = 0.09016, \quad P(\text{Healthy}) = 0.90984$. $$P(\text{Test}_2^+) = (0.99 \times 0.09016) + (0.01 \times 0.90984) = 0.089258 + 0.009098 = \mathbf{0.098356}$$
2. Sequential Posterior: $$P(\text{Sick} \mid \text{Test}_1^+, \text{Test}_2^+) = \frac{0.089258}{0.098356} \approx \mathbf{90.8\%}!$$ Starting from a tiny $0.1\%$ prevalence, two positive tests boost confidence to $>90\%$!

Problem 3: Naive Bayes Math & Laplace Smoothing from Scratch

Challenging

🧒 The Context: Timmy trains a spam filter on 4 Spam and 6 Ham emails. Word "lottery" is in 3 Spam, 0 Ham. Word "lunch" is in 0 Spam, 4 Ham.

  1. Explain why raw Naive Bayes fails on an email containing "lottery" and "lunch".
  2. Apply Laplace Smoothing ($\alpha = 1, |V| = 2$) and compute smoothed posterior $P(\text{Spam} \mid \text{Email})$.
Click to view solution for Problem 3
1. The Zero-Probability Disaster: $P(\text{"lunch"} \mid \text{Spam}) = 0$ and $P(\text{"lottery"} \mid \text{Ham}) = 0$. Both class scores multiply to zero ($0/0$ crash!).
2. Laplace Smoothing ($\alpha = 1, |V| = 2$):
  • $\text{Score}(\text{Spam}) = 0.40 \times \frac{3+1}{4+2} \times \frac{0+1}{4+2} = 0.40 \times \frac{4}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} \approx \mathbf{0.0444}$
  • $\text{Score}(\text{Ham}) = 0.60 \times \frac{0+1}{6+2} \times \frac{4+1}{6+2} = 0.60 \times \frac{1}{8} \times \frac{5}{8} \approx \mathbf{0.0469}$
$$P(\text{Spam} \mid \text{Email}) = \frac{0.0444}{0.0444 + 0.0469} \approx \mathbf{48.6\%}, \quad P(\text{Ham}) \approx \mathbf{51.4\%}$$ Laplace smoothing recovers a sensible, balanced decision without dividing by zero!
