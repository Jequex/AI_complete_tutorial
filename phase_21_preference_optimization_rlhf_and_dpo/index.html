Phase 21

Preference Optimization (RLHF & DPO)

From Supervised Next-Token Prediction to Human-Aligned Value Optimization

Architectural Paradigm

Supervised Fine-Tuning teaches language models format and conversational turn-taking, but treats all demonstrations as equally optimal. Phase 21 covers the mathematics, algorithms, and engineering of aligning LLMs with nuanced human values: the Bradley-Terry pairwise preference model, neural Reward Modeling $[d_{\text{model}} \to 1]$, classic 4-model RLHF with PPO and GAE, Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) eliminating middleman models, Kahneman-Tversky Optimization (KTO) for unpaired feedback, and Online Iterative alignment.

01. Human Preference Modeling & The Bradley-Terry Framework

Why human scalar rating (1-10) is noisy and uncalibrated, and why pairwise comparison ($y_w \succ y_l \mid x$) is mathematically robust. The Bradley-Terry logistic model (1952), Thurstonian probit comparison, Plackett-Luce ranking generalization for $K > 2$ outputs, and transitivity proofs.

02. Reward Modeling Architecture & Pairwise Loss Derivation

Replacing the causal LM vocabulary head with a scalar regression head $[d_{\text{model}} \to 1]$. Pairwise ranking loss derivation, terminal token causal extraction, score normalization and centering, and diagnosing Reward Hacking / Goodhart's Law (length bias, sycophancy).

03. RLHF & PPO Policy Gradients

The complete 4-model memory topology ($\pi_\theta, \pi_{\text{ref}}, r_\phi, V_\psi$), VRAM arithmetic, token-level KL divergence penalties preventing policy collapse, Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) clipped surrogate objective, and Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE).

04. Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

The Rafailov et al. breakthrough: analytical closed-form mapping between optimal policy and reward, algebraic cancellation of the intractable partition function $Z(x)$, eliminating Reward and Critic models, and dynamic error-weighting gradient dynamics.

05. KTO (Kahneman-Tversky Optimization) & Online Preference Alignment

Moving beyond paired datasets: optimizing from unpaired binary telemetry (thumbs-up / thumbs-down) using Prospect Theory loss aversion. Reference point $z_{\text{ref}}$ dynamics, and overcoming offline distribution shift with Online / Iterative DPO loops.
