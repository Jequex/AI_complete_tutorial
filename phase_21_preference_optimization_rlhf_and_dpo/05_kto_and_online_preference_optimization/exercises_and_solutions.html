Problem 1: Prospect Theory & Asymmetric Loss Weighting (Easy)

In KTO, evaluate the loss for desirable output ($\Delta z = +1.0$) vs undesirable output ($\Delta z = -1.0$) with $\lambda_{\text{undesirable}} = 1.50$.

View Complete Solution

Step 1: $\ell_D = 1 - \sigma(1.0) = 1 - 0.7311 = 0.2689$.

Step 2: $\ell_U = 1 - \sigma(1.0) = 0.2689$. Multiplied by $\lambda_{\text{undesirable}} = 1.50$, the loss contribution is $0.4034$.

Step 3: The undesirable output is penalized $1.5\times$ more heavily, enforcing risk-averse behavior on harmful or low-quality generations.

Problem 2: Reference Point $z_{\text{ref}}$ Dynamics & Running Estimates (Medium)

  1. What is $z_{\text{ref}}$ at epoch 0 when $\pi_\theta = \pi_{\text{ref}}$?
  2. What happens if $z_{\text{ref}}$ is kept static at 0.0 while policy reward drifts to $+1.5$?
  3. How does an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) maintain calibration?
View Complete Solution

Step 1: $z_{\text{ref}}^{(0)} = \beta \log(1.0) = 0.0$.

Step 2: If $z_{\text{ref}}$ is frozen, an undesirable output with reward $+1.0$ (bad compared to the $+1.5$ average) is mistakenly treated as a gain relative to $0.0$, corrupting training.

Step 3: EMA tracks $z_{\text{ref}}^{(t)} = \alpha z_{\text{ref}}^{(t-1)} + (1-\alpha) \bar{z}_{\text{batch}}$, keeping the baseline centered on the model's current capability.

Problem 3: Unpaired Telemetry vs. Paired Annotation Economics (Medium)

A production system serves 1M queries/day with a 2% thumbs-up/down click rate. Compare this to a $200k/year budget for paid paired contractor annotations at $0.80/pair.

View Complete Solution

Paid contractors produce $200{,}000 / 0.80 = 250{,}000$ pairs.

Unpaired telemetry generates $1{,}000{,}000 \times 0.02 \times 365 = 7{,}300{,}000$ binary examples ($29.2\times$ more data for free!), providing massive sample efficiency via KTO.

Problem 4: Distribution Shift in Offline DPO vs. Online Self-Correction (Hard)

How does Online / Iterative DPO eliminate persistent bad habits (e.g. unsolicited disclaimers) that offline DPO cannot correct?

View Complete Solution

In offline DPO, static pairs only contain mistakes made by the old SFT model. New habits developed during training never appear in the negative dataset and go unpenalized.

Online DPO samples responses directly from the active policy $\pi_\theta$, catches the new disclaimer in real-time with an LLM judge, and applies an immediate on-policy corrective gradient.
