Topic 04: MCP Transports & Session Lifecycle

Handshake state machines, capability negotiation, ping heartbeats, and Stdio vs. SSE transports.

1. The 12-Year-Old Intuitive Analogy: Diplomatic Passport Control & Handshakes

Imagine arriving at an international embassy to do business. You cannot kick the doors open and begin shouting commands.

First, you present your credentials and agree on a common language (Initialize). Next, the ambassador issues your clearance badge (Capabilities). You acknowledge the clearance (Notifications/Initialized), and only then do the security gates unlock. Periodic check-ins (Ping/Pong) verify you haven't fainted, and a polite sign-off closes the gate when you leave.

2. Complete Protocol Symbol Glossary

Symbol / Term Protocol Representation Description
$\text{initialize}$ Handshake Request Client sends version and capabilities; server responds with supported features.
$\mathcal{N}_{\text{initialized}}$ Handshake Notification Client acknowledgment. Must be delivered before standard RPC operations are allowed.
$\text{ping}$ Liveness Probe Heartbeat request; returns {} to guarantee channel connectivity.
$\mathcal{C}_{\text{capabilities}}$ Capability Manifest Feature bitmask specifying support for tools, resources, prompts, roots, and sampling.
