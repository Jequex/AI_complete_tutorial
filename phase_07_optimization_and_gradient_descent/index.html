Optimization & Gradient Descent

Optimization is the algorithmic heartbeat of artificial intelligence. It dictates how neural networks navigate hyper-dimensional loss landscapes, escape ill-conditioned ravines, calibrate per-parameter learning rates, and converge to robust, generalizable solutions.

The foggy mountain descent. First-order steepest descent proofs, Batch GD vs pure SGD vs Mini-batch SGD, variance scaling law ($\sigma^2 / B$), Lipschitz stability limits ($\eta < 2/L$), gradient noise temperature, and modern cosine annealing schedules.

The smart all-terrain hiking shoes. Why uniform step sizes fail on sparse features, AdaGrad's monotonic freezing flaw, RMSProp exponential smoothing, complete mathematical derivation of Adam's bias corrections, and the AdamW decoupled weight decay proof.
