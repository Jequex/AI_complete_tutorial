Phase 14

Computer Vision: CNNs to Vision Transformers

From 2D discrete convolutions, feature maps, and spatial pooling hierarchies to ResNet's gradient highway and the modern Vision Transformer (ViT) self-attention paradigm.

01. Convolutions & Feature Maps

2D discrete convolution vs cross-correlation, spatial output dimension arithmetic ($O = \lfloor \frac{W - K + 2P}{S} \rfloor + 1$), multi-channel tensor convolution ($[C_{\text{out}}, C_{\text{in}}, K_h, K_w]$), FLOPs/MACs bookkeeping, and recursive receptive field propagation ($RF_l = RF_{l-1} + (k_l - 1) J_{l-1}$).

02. Pooling & Spatial Hierarchies

Spatial downsampling mechanics: MaxPooling2D, AvgPooling2D, and Global Average Pooling (GAP). Invariance vs. equivariance, argmax routing masks in backpropagation, uniform gradient distribution, and how GAP eliminates 99.5% of classification head parameters.

📖 Lecture Document ✏️ Exercises & Solutions Markdown Source

03. Classic CNN Architectures: LeNet, VGG, and ResNet

Architectural evolution from LeNet-5 to AlexNet and VGGNet. The $3 \times 3$ stacking advantage (28% parameter reduction over $5 \times 5$). The Degradation Problem (higher training error in deep plain networks), ResNet formulation ($\mathbf{y} = \mathcal{F}(\mathbf{x}) + \mathbf{x}$), and the mathematical proof of the unhindered Gradient Highway ($\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{x}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{y}}(\frac{\partial \mathcal{F}}{\partial \mathbf{x}} + \mathbf{I})$).

📖 Lecture Document ✏️ Exercises & Solutions Markdown Source

04. Vision Transformers (ViT)

The visual paradigm shift: "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words". Non-overlapping patch extraction ($N = \frac{HW}{P^2}$), linear projection to dimension $D$, prepending the learnable [CLS] token, 1D positional embeddings, Multi-Head Self-Attention, and the trade-off between convolutional inductive bias and foundation-scale Transformer capacity.
