Topic 18.4: Modern LLM Architectures

Grouped-Query Attention (GQA), SwiGLU Gated Feedforward Networks, and FlashAttention IO-Aware Tiling.

👨‍🍳 12-Year-Old Analogy: The Chef's High-Speed Cutting Board & The Shared Kitchen Tools

In a 64-chef Michelin-star banquet kitchen:

  • Grouped-Query Attention (Shared Kitchen Tools): Instead of giving all 64 chefs their own individual pepper grinder (cluttering the counter with 64 grinders), we group the chefs into 8 teams of 8 chefs. Each team shares 1 pepper grinder. Clutter drops by $8\times$, cooking speeds up, and the food tastes just as delicious!
  • SwiGLU (The Smart Valve): Rather than a crude on/off blender switch, SwiGLU is a continuous sensor valve that lets through exactly the right amount of sweetness while dynamically filtering out bitterness.
  • FlashAttention (The Cutting Board vs. The Cold Basement): Instead of walking down two flights of stairs to the cold basement (GPU HBM memory) to write on a giant $10,000 \times 10,000$ whiteboard after every carrot slice, the chef keeps the vegetables right on the cutting board (on-chip SRAM), computes running totals in their head (online softmax), and finishes $4\times$ faster!

2. Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) & The KV-Cache

Autoregressive generation stores past keys and values in memory: $$\text{KV-Cache Size} = 2 \times L \times T \times H_{KV} \times d_{head} \times 2 \text{ bytes}$$

3. SwiGLU Activation & Dimension Scaling

SwiGLU replaces traditional linear activations with multiplicative gating: $$\text{SwiGLU}(x) = \left( (x W_{gate}) \odot \text{SiLU}(x W_{up}) \right) W_{down}$$

Because SwiGLU uses 3 projection matrices instead of 2, parameter parity with a standard $4 d_{model}$ MLP requires scaling the intermediate dimension: $$3 \cdot d_{model} \cdot d_{ffn} = 8 d_{model}^2 \implies d_{ffn} = \frac{8}{3} d_{model} \approx 2.667 d_{model}$$ For $d_{model} = 4096$, rounding to the nearest multiple of 256 yields $d_{ffn} = \mathbf{11,008}$.

4. FlashAttention & Online Softmax Normalization

Standard attention incurs $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ memory reads and writes by materializing the full attention matrix $P \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times T}$ in GPU HBM.

FlashAttention eliminates this bottleneck by tiling computation inside fast SRAM using Online Softmax:

$$m^{\text{new}} = \max(m^{(1)}, m^{(2)})$$ $$d^{\text{new}} = d^{(1)} e^{m^{(1)} - m^{\text{new}}} + d^{(2)} e^{m^{(2)} - m^{\text{new}}}$$ $$O^{\text{new}} = O^{(1)} e^{m^{(1)} - m^{\text{new}}} + \left(\sum_j e^{x_j^{(2)} - m^{\text{new}}} V_j^{(2)}\right)$$

HBM memory traffic drops from $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}(T)$, achieving an exact mathematical result with $2\times - 4\times$ speedup.
